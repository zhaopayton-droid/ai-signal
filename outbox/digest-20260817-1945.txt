**Paper2 | Handover of In-Context Learning State Across Session Boundaries** — 2026-08-15 01:47 北京时间
Problem: When an LLM-based task must continue in a new session — context limit hit, app restart, or handing off to another agent — the system has to decide what information to carry forward, with no principled theory for what's actually needed.
问题:当基于 LLM 的任务因上下文超限、应用重启或交接给另一个智能体而必须在新会话中继续时,系统需要决定该传递哪些信息,而目前缺乏一套关于"到底需要保留什么"的理论。
Approach: The authors formalize handover as transferring a task-relative in-context-learning state, and propose a three-part record: exact decisions/constraints, task-justified statistics for repeated evidence, and raw observations that summary statistics can't preserve.
方法:作者将"交接"形式化为传递与任务相关的上下文学习状态,并提出一种三段式记录方案:精确保留决策与约束、用面向任务的统计量概括重复证据、保留统计量无法覆盖的原始观测。
Result: For Gaussian linear regression the handover is exact and finite-dimensional with provable bit-budget bounds; for nonparametric regression they derive bounds relating memory size to prediction error.
结果:在高斯线性回归场景下,交接可以做到精确且有限维,并给出可证明的比特预算上界;在非参数回归场景下,给出了记忆量与预测误差之间关系的上下界。
Why it matters: Directly relevant to anyone building long-running or multi-agent systems that must survive context limits or agent handoffs — a theoretical basis for "what to put in the memory file."
为什么重要:对任何需要构建长时间运行或多智能体交接系统的团队都有直接参考价值,为"记忆文件里到底该写什么"提供了理论依据。

Result: Twin clears 179 of 183 levels (97.8%), beating human efficiency on 88.3% of cleared levels, and infers the goal before any reward signal on 87.2% of them; a base model alone scores 7.8%, an off-the-shelf harness raises it to 61.1%, and Twin's world model raises the same base model to 93.3%.

**Paper7 | Split the Labor: Separating Evidence Interpretation from Decision Aggregation** — 2026-08-15 01:24 北京时间
Problem: Systems that ask an LLM to reach a conclusion from many sources usually dump everything into one prompt, conflating "interpreting a source" (rewards context) with "combining interpretations" (rewards fixed arithmetic) — which the authors show causes a specific failure they call count-scale drift.
问题:让 LLM 综合多个信息源得出结论的系统,通常把所有内容一股脑塞进一个 prompt,混淆了"解读单个信息源"(需要更强的上下文理解)与"汇总多个解读结果"(需要固定的算术规则)这两种不同性质的操作,作者指出这会导致一种他们称为"计数尺度漂移"的特定失效模式。
Approach: They propose a four-field evidence tuple (hypothesis, reliability bucket, rationale, provenance) that separates the two operations, and show that pooling calibrated log-likelihood ratios — rather than thresholding a sum of unnormalized weights — fixes the drift.
方法:他们提出一种四要素证据元组(假设、可信度分桶、理由、来源),将两种操作分离开来,并证明汇总"校准后的对数似然比"(而非对未归一化权重求和后设阈值)可以修正这种漂移问题。
Result: Instantiated on a longitudinal corpus both after and before outcomes resolve, the approach reaches 0.921 AUPRC versus 0.805 for a hand-crafted baseline; the authors also state five falsifiable predictions and three negative results.
结果:在一个纵向语料库上(分别在结果揭晓前后)实测,该方法达到 0.921 的 AUPRC,而人工设计的基线方法为 0.805;作者还给出了五条可证伪的预测和三项负面结果。
Why it matters: A practical fix — "arithmetic rather than architectural" — for a common bug in multi-source LLM pipelines (RAG, evidence aggregation, panel-of-judges setups), and the same fix applies beyond LLMs to any score-summing detection system.
为什么重要:这是一个"改算法而非改架构"的实用修复方案,针对的是多信息源 LLM 系统(RAG、证据聚合、多评审员打分等)中的一个常见 bug,且该修复方法不局限于 LLM,也适用于任何基于加权求和打分的检测系统。

问题:面对推理任务的冻结 LLM 存在两个耦合的弱点:一是没有充分利用自身残差流中已经编码的证据,二是无法察觉输入信息不足,从而"编造"答案而非拒答;而现有分别解决这两个问题的方法在同一次前向传播中会相互干扰。

**Paper10 | More Correct Mass, Worse Answers: Why Power Sampling Can Fail and How to Fix It** — 2026-08-15 00:01 北京时间
Problem: Power Sampling — a verifier-free way to sharpen an LLM's output distribution toward correct trajectories at inference time — can paradoxically make downstream reasoning worse even while moving more probability mass to correct answers, with accuracy drops of up to 18.5 points observed with self-consistency.
问题:Power Sampling 是一种在推理阶段无需验证器、把 LLM 输出分布向正确轨迹"锐化"的方法,但作者发现一个悖论:它虽然能把更多概率质量分配到正确轨迹上,却可能让下游推理效果反而变差,在自洽性(self-consistency)场景下观察到准确率最多下降 18.5 个百分点。
Approach: The authors trace this to two mismatches — "dose mismatch" (a fixed exponent causes wildly different amounts of sharpening across problems) and "coverage mismatch" (global sharpening kills the diversity of reasoning paths needed for downstream aggregation) — and propose a deformation-controlled, support-preserving Power target that calibrates sharpening per problem.
