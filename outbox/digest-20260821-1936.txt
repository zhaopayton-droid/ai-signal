**Paper1 | ConceptGuard: Benchmarking Context-Sensitive Unlearning in Large Language Models — 2026-08-21 01:59 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338v1

Problem: Existing LLM unlearning benchmarks test forgetting with disjoint fact sets and simple recall, missing the real requirement of removing harmful uses of a concept while keeping its benign uses intact. Approach: ConceptGuard introduces "dual-use concepts" — ideas usable both harmfully and benignly — and builds forget/retain sets that are explicitly complementary at the concept level, with intent-sensitive evaluation. Result: Current unlearning methods perform poorly under this test, showing weak separation between harmful and benign contexts and a strong forgetting-utility trade-off, indicating today's unlearning techniques are less robust than fact-level benchmarks suggest.

问题:现有 LLM "遗忘"(unlearning)基准用互不相关的事实集合和简单事实回忆来测试遗忘效果,未能覆盖真正的需求——既要清除某个概念的有害用法,又要保留其正常有益用法。方法:ConceptGuard 提出"双用概念"(即既可用于有害场景也可用于正常场景的概念),构建在概念层面互补的遗忘集/保留集,并采用对意图敏感的评估方式。结果:现有遗忘方法在该测试下表现不佳,在有害与良性场景之间的区分度很弱,且存在明显的"遗忘-可用性"权衡,说明当前遗忘技术的鲁棒性比基于事实的基准所显示的要差。

https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20326v1

Approach: The authors propose TCPα, a new confidence target with a margin-controlled penalty for misclassified samples that provably separates correct from incorrect predictions by a margin independent of class count. **Paper6 | AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement — 2026-08-21 01:56 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318v1

Problem: Recursive self-improvement — an AI improving the training algorithm that builds the next AI — hinges on whether an agent can actually redesign training algorithms, but no benchmark isolates that specific skill from data-collection or hyperparameter tuning. Approach: AI4AI-Bench gives an agent 4 hours on one B300 GPU to rewrite a frozen research repository's training algorithm across 10 algorithm families, then reruns the new code from scratch (up to 12 hours) and scores it on a 0-1 scale versus the shipped algorithm (0.1) and the task optimum (1.0). 问题:"递归自我改进"——AI 改进用于训练下一代 AI 的训练算法本身——的可行性,取决于智能体是否真的能重新设计训练算法,但目前没有基准把这项能力从"收集数据"或"调超参数"中单独剥离出来衡量。方法:AI4AI-Bench 让智能体在一块 B300 GPU 上用 4 小时改写一个冻结研究代码库(覆盖 10 个训练算法家族)的训练算法,然后从零重跑新代码(最长 12 小时),按 0-1 量表打分:0 代表无信息量的模型,0.1 代表代码库原有算法水平,1.0 代表该任务的理论最优。结果:在 6 个系统的 29 种配置中,平均得分仅为 0.166,最好的系统也只达到 0.250——大多数提交根本没有真正改变模型的学习方式,少数真正改动学习方式的提交平均得 0.226(其余为 0.126),而更多的推理算力主要是让模型更愿意尝试真正的算法改动,而非保证成功。

**Paper7 | Pandora's AI Model Routing Box: Efficient Allocation with Costly Value Estimation — 2026-08-21 01:54 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20316v1

Problem: Routing queries to the best/cheapest specialist model requires estimating each specialist's expected value, but accurate value estimation itself costs money (e.g. running retrieval or partial reasoning), creating a cost/accuracy tradeoff in the router itself. Approach: The authors formalize this as a Pandora's Box search problem; under a Gaussian signal model they derive closed-form value-of-information rules ("Pandora's Router") for when it's worth paying for a better estimate, and extend it to a decentralized "Pandora's Bidder" setting where specialists themselves choose whether to self-assess before bidding for a query. Result: Across three domains (multi-LLM benchmarks, retrieval-augmented specialists, and variable-reasoning-effort LLMs), Pandora's Router matches exhaustive-estimation routing quality while calling the expensive estimator far less often; in the decentralized setting, value-of-information reasoning helps overall efficiency when competing estimates are accurate but can be gamed by strategic specialists when estimates are noisy.

问题:把查询路由给最合适/最便宜的专家模型,需要先估计每个专家的预期价值,但精确的价值估计本身就有成本(比如要跑检索或部分推理),这就在"路由器"自身内部形成了成本与准确度的权衡。方法:作者将这一问题形式化为经典的"潘多拉魔盒"搜索问题;在高斯信号模型下,推导出何时值得为更精确估计付出成本的闭式"信息价值"规则(称为 Pandora's Router),并将其扩展到去中心化场景 Pandora's Bidder——由各专家自行决定是否在竞价接单前先做自我评估。结果:在三个领域(多 LLM 基准、检索增强型专家、可变推理算力的 LLM)上,Pandora's Router 能达到与"穷举式估计"相当的路由质量,同时大幅减少调用昂贵估计器的次数;在去中心化场景中,当各方估计较准确时,信息价值推理能提升整体效率,但当估计较嘈杂时,可能被有策略的专家用来为自己谋利。

**Paper10 | Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null — 2026-08-21 01:30 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290v1

Problem: Judging whether a language model has "self-improved" increasingly relies on tracking which individual problems it gains or loses, not just mean accuracy — but this differencing of two noisy estimates is highly vulnerable to measurement artifacts that can fabricate or invert findings. Approach: The authors audit three rounds of LoRA self-training on Qwen3-8B against a frozen control run through the identical pipeline, identify seven measurement failures (e.g. a single greedy-decode ledger manufacturing fake capability changes), and replace the standard threshold test with a per-problem exact test against a pooled baseline under false-discovery-rate control. Result: The corrected audit finds that external distillation genuinely improves problems the base model rarely reaches, while three forms of self-training do not — and on problems the base model never reaches, evidence for self-training gains is inconclusive, while self-training measurably corrupts previously-solved problems above the measurement noise floor.

问题:判断一个语言模型是否真的"自我提升",越来越依赖于追踪它在哪些具体题目上"进步"或"退步",而不只是看平均准确率——但这种对两个带噪声估计量做差分的方法,极易受测量假象影响,可能捏造甚至颠倒结论。方法:作者审计了在 Qwen3-8B 上进行的三轮 LoRA 自我训练,并与经过完全相同流程处理的冻结对照组做对比,发现了七种测量失误(例如仅凭一次贪婪解码就能在未训练模型上"制造"出虚假的能力变化),并用一种在虚假发现率(FDR)控制下、针对每道题的精确检验替代了经不起重复验证的标准阈值检验。结果:经过校正的审计发现,外部蒸馏确实能提升基座模型很少能答对的题目,而三种自我训练方法都做不到;而在基座模型完全答不出的题目上,自我训练是否真的有效尚无定论,同时自我训练会以明显高于测量噪声下限的比率,"损坏"那些原本已经答对的题目。

**Paper11 | Inject, Align, Recover: Staged Post-Training for Retrieval-Free Document Knowledge Internalization — 2026-08-21 01:14 北京时间**
