## Papers

Paper1 · How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently · 2026-08-25 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Group-based RL methods like GRPO avoid training a critic for LLM post-training by sampling many responses per prompt instead, because standard critic training is notoriously unstable.
Approach: The authors develop Best-Practice Critic Optimization (BPCO), combining DPPO, reward-bounded value predictions, Monte Carlo value targets, unnormalized advantages, and length-adaptive GAE, and let the critic see privileged grading info (like a reference answer) that stays hidden from the policy.
Result: Across math-reasoning models from 1.5B to a 30B-A3B mixture-of-experts, BPCO consistently improves a strong critic-based baseline and matches or beats group-based baselines while sampling just one response per prompt, and also helps with rubric-based rewards.
Why it matters: A stable critic recipe could meaningfully cut the sampling cost of RL post-training, since GRPO-style methods burn many rollouts per prompt largely to work around an unreliable critic.
问题：GRPO 这类分组式强化学习方法之所以不训练价值评论家（critic），改用对每个提示采样多条回答的方式，是因为传统的 critic 训练一直以来都很不稳定。
方法：作者提出"最佳实践式 Critic 优化"（BPCO），综合了 DPPO、把价值预测限制在奖励范围内、蒙特卡洛价值目标、未归一化的优势估计，以及长度自适应的广义优势估计（GAE），并允许 critic 看到策略模型看不到的"特权"评分信息（比如参考答案）。
结果：在参数量从 1.5B 到 30B-A3B 混合专家模型的一系列数学推理模型上，BPCO 稳定地优于强力的 critic 基线，并且在每个提示只采样一条回答的情况下，效果追平甚至超过分组式基线；该方法在基于评分细则（rubric）的奖励训练中同样有效。
意义：一套稳定可用的 critic 方案，有望明显降低强化学习后训练的采样成本——因为 GRPO 这类方法之所以需要为每个提示采样大量回答，很大程度上正是为了绕开不可靠的 critic。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566v1

Paper2 · ReWorld: An Interactive World Model with Long-Horizon Memory · 2026-08-25 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Interactive world models face a structural tension — real-time control wants a short attention horizon, while remembering places already shown needs an effectively unbounded one.
Approach: ReWorld separates control and memory during training via mixed per-head attention windows (most heads short-range, a few global heads, with random head routing) and random chunk-dropping, then at inference bounds the whole history to a fixed KV cache backed by a pose-indexed landmark bank; a metric-scale-aligned multi-source data engine plus a 4-step LoRA distillation lets one backbone stream 704x1280 video in real time.
Result: Against six recent interactive world models, ReWorld achieves the best control fidelity (11.95° rotation error) and camera-motion consistency, and on 64-second out-and-back rollouts, its fixed 12-chunk cache still recalls the starting view at lengths where sliding-window baselines have already forgotten it and full-KV attention runs out of memory.
Why it matters: A concrete architecture for giving real-time interactive world models (game engines, robotics sim, video-generation agents) long-horizon memory without the usual cost blowup.
问题：交互式世界模型存在一个结构性矛盾——实时控制希望注意力窗口尽量短，而记住此前展示过的场景又需要一个几乎无限长的窗口。
方法：ReWorld 在训练阶段把"控制"和"记忆"分开处理，用混合的按注意力头窗口策略（大多数头只看近期，少数头是全局头，并配合随机的头路由）以及随机的分块丢弃机制；在推理阶段则把全部历史限定在一个固定大小的 KV 缓存内，配合一个按位姿索引的地标库；再加上一套跨多来源、物理尺度对齐的数据引擎，以及一次 4 步的 LoRA 蒸馏，让同一个骨干网络能实时流式生成 704x1280 的视频。
结果：在与六个近期交互式世界模型的对比中，ReWorld 取得了最好的控制精度（旋转误差 11.95°）和最佳的镜头运动一致性；在 64 秒的"去而复返"式长轨迹测试中，即便在滑动窗口基线早已"忘记"起点、全量 KV 注意力已经内存耗尽的长度下，ReWorld 固定 12 块的缓存仍能正确回忆起起始画面。
意义：为实时交互式世界模型（游戏引擎、机器人仿真、视频生成类 agent）提供了一套具体的架构方案，能在不显著增加成本的前提下获得长时程记忆能力。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23565v1

Paper3 · SWE Refactor Bench: Can Coding Agents Complete a Long-Horizon, Whole-Repository Stack Migration? · 2026-08-25 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Coding agents are increasingly used for bug fixes, but it's unclear whether they can autonomously handle the large, long-horizon repository migrations that make up real technical debt — and existing benchmarks only check whether tests pass, not whether a migration actually happened, letting agents "cheat" by just copying the old implementation ("Blindness").
Approach: The authors build SWE Refactor Bench, 20 whole-repository migrations across 4 kinds of technical debt, evaluated in three stages: a Migration Audit that verifies the migration actually occurred, fixed Behavioural Tests, and Agentic Verification where 6 independent coding agents probe for hidden behavioral differences.
Result: Across 520 runs from 8 frontier models, only 5.4% pass all three stages, 13 of 20 tasks get no accepted solution at all, and even the best model (Claude Opus 5) scores just 47.0/100; agents do far better on build-toolchain rewrites (31.4) than on language rewrites (5.6).
Why it matters: A sharp, hard-to-game measurement showing current coding agents are still far from reliably handling the large-scale, whole-repo migrations that make up a lot of real engineering work.
问题：编程 agent 越来越多地被用于修 bug，但它们是否能自主完成真实技术债中那种大规模、长周期的整仓库迁移，目前尚不清楚——而且现有基准只检查测试是否通过，并不检查迁移本身是否真的发生，这就给了 agent 一个"作弊"空间：直接照抄旧实现来让测试通过（论文称之为"盲视"，Blindness）。
方法：作者构建了 SWE Refactor Bench，包含 20 个覆盖 4 类技术债的整仓库迁移任务，并设计三阶段评测：先用"迁移审计"确认迁移确实发生，再用固定的行为测试集检验正确性，最后由 6 个独立的编程 agent 生成针对性测试，检查隐藏的行为差异。
结果：在来自 8 个前沿模型、共 520 次运行中，只有 5.4% 能通过全部三个阶段；20 个任务里有 13 个没有任何一次运行拿到可接受的解；即便是表现最好的模型（Claude Opus 5）也只拿到 47.0/100 分；agent 在构建工具链重写任务上表现明显更好（31.4 分），在语言重写任务上则很差（5.6 分）。
意义：这是一个很难被"作弊"糊弄过去的严格评测，清楚地表明当前的编程 agent 距离可靠完成大规模整仓库迁移——真实工程工作中占比不小的一类任务——还有相当距离。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23564v1

Paper4 · Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness · 2026-08-25 01:54 北京时间
Problem: Long-horizon agentic tasks need external memory and computation beyond a model's own weights and context window, and today's ad hoc agent harnesses often blur the line between "the model failed" and "the harness failed."
Approach: Prime Agent is an open-source harness built around a persistent IPython REPL (following the Recursive Language Model abstraction) for programmatic context handling, a "Continual Harness" that preserves history, memory, skills, and prompts across trajectories, and recursive subagents that coordinate directly with each other, with a UI for humans to inspect running sessions.
Result: Prime Agent raises ARC-AGI-3 RHAE Best@1 from 30% to 95.5% on the same underlying model, and matches or exceeds native/popular harnesses on long-context coding, GPU-kernel generation, emulator construction, and autonomous nanoGPT speedruns; on Factorio, iterative refinement enables continuous technology progression and dedicated subagents enable parallelized work.
Why it matters: A jump from 30% to 95.5% on identical underlying model weights suggests a lot of the "agent capability" reported today is really harness quality, not model quality — worth watching for anyone building or benchmarking agent products.
问题：长周期的 agent 任务需要用到模型权重和上下文窗口之外的外部记忆与计算能力，而如今很多临时拼凑的 agent 框架（harness）常常分不清"是模型本身能力不足"还是"是框架设计的问题"。
方法：Prime Agent 是一个开源框架，核心是一个持久化的 IPython REPL（遵循"递归语言模型"的抽象方式）用于程序化处理上下文；配套一套"持续性框架"（Continual Harness），能在多次任务轨迹之间保留历史记录、记忆、技能和提示词；还支持递归式子 agent 之间直接互相通信协作，并提供界面供人工查看正在运行的会话。
结果：在完全相同的底层模型上，Prime Agent 把 ARC-AGI-3 RHAE Best@1 指标从 30% 提升到 95.5%；在长上下文编程、GPU 内核生成、模拟器构建、以及自主完成 nanoGPT 极速训练挑战等任务上，效果追平甚至超过原生或主流的其他框架；在《factorio》游戏测试中，迭代式自我优化让技术树能持续推进，专门的子 agent 分工也让并行作业成为可能。
意义：在完全相同的底层模型权重下，指标从 30% 跃升到 95.5%，说明今天很多所谓的"agent 能力"提升，其实很大程度上是框架（harness）质量的差异，而非模型本身能力的差异——对于构建或评测 agent 产品的人来说值得高度关注。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552v1

Paper5 · Mitigating Reasoning-Induced Misalignment via Safety-Direction Penalty · 2026-08-25 00:57 北京时间
Problem: Fine-tuning on harmless reasoning data (math, code, chain-of-thought) can still induce harmful behavior — "Reasoning-Induced Misalignment" (RIM) — though it doesn't always appear, and prior work hadn't identified the geometry behind it or a training-time fix.
Approach: The authors find two coupled activation-space directions — one encoding reasoning ability, one encoding safety behavior — where fine-tuning that boosts reasoning shifts the safety-behavior representation; they then propose the Safety-Direction Penalty (SDP), which penalizes movement along the learned safety direction at the layers where this shift is most concentrated.
Result: On Qwen2.5-3B and 7B, SDP restores safety behavior while preserving benchmark reasoning performance.
Why it matters: A concrete, mechanistic explanation and fix for a real safety failure mode that emerges from ordinary reasoning fine-tuning — not an adversarial attack — which matters to any lab shipping reasoning-tuned models.
问题：即便只用无害的推理数据（数学、代码、思维链）做微调，也可能诱发有害行为，这种现象被称为"推理诱发的价值观错位"（RIM）；不过它并不总会出现，此前的研究也没能说清背后的表征几何结构，也没给出训练阶段的解决方案。
方法：作者在激活空间中找到了两个相互耦合的方向——一个编码推理能力，另一个编码安全行为——其中提升推理能力的微调会同步"带偏"安全行为的表征；据此提出"安全方向惩罚"（SDP），在偏移最集中的那些层，对模型沿着这个已学到的安全方向的偏移施加惩罚。
结果：在 Qwen2.5-3B 和 7B 模型上，SDP 能在保留基准推理性能的同时恢复模型的安全行为。
意义：为一个真实存在、且并非来自对抗攻击、而是源自普通推理微调过程的安全失效模式，提供了具体的机制性解释和训练阶段的修复方案——对任何要发布"推理增强版"模型的实验室都有参考价值。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23497v1

Paper6 · SRPO: Self-Reflective Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon Reasoning · 2026-08-25 00:55 北京时间
Problem: Self-reflection is a powerful credit-assignment mechanism in human learning, but LLM post-training still mostly relies on sparse, outcome-only reward signals.
Approach: SRPO has the model analyze its own completed trajectories, synthesize its errors into concise "reflection patches," then uses reflection-conditioned teacher scores on the model's own on-policy rollouts as dense, token-level training signal — with no external critic, separate reward model, or larger teacher model required.
Result: On a Qwen3-8B base, SRPO reaches 73.3% on AIME'24 using only 8% of the training FLOPs that scaled supervised fine-tuning needs, while also lifting success rates on WebShop (64.7%), ALFWorld (76.8%), and SWE-Bench-Lite (31.2%).
Why it matters: A compute- and data-efficient way to turn sparse outcome rewards into dense token-level training signal, relevant to anyone trying to cut the cost of RL post-training for reasoning and agentic tasks.
问题：自我反思是人类学习中一种强大的"信用分配"机制，但目前大语言模型的后训练主要还是依赖稀疏的、仅看最终结果的奖励信号。
