Paper1 | Pass the Baton: Trajectory-Relayed On-Policy Distillation · 2026-07-29 01:59 北京时间
Problem: On-policy distillation lets a student model's own trajectory ground training, but once the student goes down a wrong reasoning path early on, everything after it is misdirected and wastes compute. Approach: Relay-OPD lets the teacher briefly take over at detected failure points to produce a short "teacher leg," then hands control back to the student, using a limited relay budget to focus intervention on critical early positions. Result: With a Qwen3-4B teacher and 0.6B/1.7B students on eight math-reasoning benchmarks, Relay-OPD beat standard on-policy distillation by +5.73% and the strongest baseline by +1.49% on average, while cutting training trajectory length by over 50%.
问题：on-policy distillation（同策略蒸馏）让学生模型用自己的生成轨迹来接受监督，但学生一旦在早期走错推理方向，后续所有生成都会被带偏，浪费大量算力。方法：Relay-OPD 在检测到失败点时让教师模型短暂"接管"生成一小段"教师段落"，之后再把控制权交还给学生，并用有限的"接力预算"把干预集中在关键的早期位置。结果：在以 Qwen3-4B 为教师、0.6B/1.7B 为学生的八个数学推理基准上，Relay-OPD 平均比标准同策略蒸馏高出5.73%，比最强基线高1.49%，同时训练轨迹长度缩短超过50%。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26057v1

Paper2 | πR²: Reactive Real-time Flow Policies · 2026-07-29 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Generalist robot manipulation policies built on large pretrained flow models run open-loop in chunks, so they can't react to new sensory input mid-execution, and replanning is too slow to fix this. Approach: πR² splits inputs into a fast proprioception channel updated every tick and a slower vision-language channel, plus a latency-adaptive flow schedule that emits one action per denoising step, letting a single trained model adapt to different hardware speeds. Result: Fine-tuned from GR00T-N1.7 on a real xArm6+XHand robot, it replans about 4x faster than the base policy (~25Hz), improving success rate by up to 23% in simulation and 30% in the real world over the strongest baseline.
问题：基于大型预训练流模型构建的通用机器人操作策略以"动作块"形式开环运行，执行过程中无法对新的传感输入做出反应，而重新规划又太慢，来不及纠正。方法：πR² 把输入拆分为每一时刻都更新的"快速本体感觉通道"和更新较慢的"视觉-语言通道"，并配合一种随硬件延迟自适应的流式调度方案，每次去噪步骤只输出一个动作，使同一个训练好的模型能适配不同硬件的响应速度。结果：在真实的 xArm6+XHand 机器人平台上基于 GR00T-N1.7 微调后，重新规划速度约为基础策略的4倍（约25Hz），相比最强基线，在仿真中成功率提升最高23%，在真实世界中提升最高30%。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26055v1

Paper3 | Spend Experts Where You Are Unsure: Confidence-Adaptive Routing for Mixture-of-Experts LoRA · 2026-07-29 01:59 北京时间
Problem: MoE-LoRA setups route every token to a fixed number of experts k, which over-spends compute on easy tokens and under-serves hard ones.
