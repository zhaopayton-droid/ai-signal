Paper1 | Reasoning Core: Designing Broad Procedural Data for Completion-Supervised Reasoning Training · 2026-08-06 01:59 北京时间
Problem: procedural generators can produce reasoning problems at scale, but they're rarely used as training data for completion-supervised fine-tuning. Approach: the authors build Reasoning Core, 50 procedural generators spanning math, logic, planning, code and more, with scorers and difficulty controls, and compare it against three other procedural-data collections across four base models. Result: in the primary 3B comparison, Reasoning Core beats the no-procedural-data baseline and all three alternative collections on DROP, LogiQA and ARC-Challenge, though semantic validity alone doesn't guarantee training usefulness — compact targets and calibrated difficulty matter more.
问题:程序化生成器能够大规模产出可验证的推理题目,但很少被当作"补全式监督微调"的训练数据来研究。方法:作者构建了 Reasoning Core——50 个覆盖数学、逻辑、规划、代码等领域的程序化生成器,配有语义评分器和难度控制,并在四种基座模型设置下与另外三套程序化数据集做对比。结果:在主要的 3B 模型对比中,Reasoning Core 在 DROP、LogiQA 和 ARC-Challenge 上超过了无程序化数据的基线以及另外三套数据集;但"语义正确"本身并不保证训练有用,紧凑的目标和恰当校准的难度才是关键。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05148v1

Paper2 | Argus: A General-Purpose Agentic Runtime for Long-Horizon Reasoning · 2026-08-06 01:58 北京时间
Problem: long-horizon agentic work needs a runtime that can persist through a working approach but pivot when evidence reveals hidden constraints or a wrong objective. Approach: Argus is a persistent, self-evolving runtime with Manager/Planner/Engineer/Reviewer roles operating over durable project state, where model weights stay fixed and "learning" happens via reviewed, verification-gated runtime memory and control policy. Result: on GPT-5.5 benchmarks Argus hits ~78% on SWE-Bench Pro vs. 59% for Direct Copilot (using 1.41x the tokens), and after self-evolution mature task waves use 21% fewer solve tokens and 15% less active time than early waves, plus real-world outcomes like an upstreamed RWKV6 kernel optimization.
问题:长周期的 agentic 工作需要一种运行时——能在方向正确时坚持,又能在证据暴露隐藏约束或目标设定错误时及时调整。方法:Argus 是一个持久化、可自我演化的运行时,由 Manager/Planner/Engineer/Reviewer 四个角色围绕持久化的项目状态协作;模型权重保持不变,"学习"是通过经过审核、有验证闸门把关的运行时记忆和控制策略来实现的。结果:在 GPT-5.5 基准测试中,Argus 在 SWE-Bench Pro 上达到约 78%,而 Direct Copilot 为 59%(代价是多用 1.41 倍 token);经过自我演化后,成熟阶段的任务批次比早期批次少用 21% 的求解 token、少花 15% 的实际工作时间,还产出了实际成果,例如一个被合并进上游的 RWKV6 内核优化。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05144v1

Paper3 | OctoLong: Mid-Training On Cross-Repository Code Contexts Enhances Long-Context Modeling · 2026-08-06 01:58 北京时间
Problem: long-context training corpora are dominated by books, papers and code repos, which are finite and often lack genuinely long-distance dependencies. Approach: OctoLong uses an AST parser, language server, and package manager to recursively retrieve cross-repository code references, curating dependency-rich contexts of millions of tokens, then mid-trains OctoLong-Instruct models (600M-14B) on a ~50B-token mix including 6.2B tokens of this data. Result: against 18 open-weight long-context models, replacing just 12% of a standard context-extension corpus with OctoLong data yields substantial gains in long-range retrieval, state tracking, repo-level code understanding, and agentic tasks, while also improving short-context API usage.
问题:现有长上下文训练语料主要来自书籍、论文和代码仓库,这些资源有限,且往往缺乏真正的长距离依赖关系。方法:OctoLong 用 AST 解析器、语言服务器和包管理器递归检索跨仓库的代码引用,构建出长达数百万 token、依赖关系丰富的上下文,再用约 500 亿 token(其中 62 亿来自这批代码上下文)对 6 亿至 140 亿参数的 OctoLong-Instruct 系列做中期训练。结果:与 18 个开放权重长上下文模型对比,仅替换传统扩展语料中 12% 的内容为 OctoLong 数据,就能在长距离检索、状态跟踪、仓库级代码理解和下游 agentic 任务上带来显著提升,同时还改善了短上下文下的 API 调用能力。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05141v1

Paper4 | Toward Skill-Native LLMs: Skill Entropy for Benchmarking and Training Long-Horizon Reasoning · 2026-08-06 01:57 北京时间
Problem: long-horizon reasoning often requires switching between distinct skills mid-chain (e.g., math then planning), but existing benchmarks mostly test single skills and can't measure how well a model switches. Approach: the authors define "Skill Entropy" as a measure of switching difficulty, build Skill²-Bench (558 skills, 9 domains, three difficulty tiers), then turn skill entropy into an RL training signal (Skill-Entropy RL) that rewards correct step-level answers alongside correct skill-sequence prediction. Result: evaluating 12 models on Skill²-Bench reveals a clear skill-switching gap on higher-entropy tasks; applying Skill-Entropy RL raises Qwen3-4B-Instruct's score from 34.4% to 68.4% and Qwen3-1.7B's from 14.6% to 40.1%, beating competitive baselines.
问题:长链条推理常常需要在中途切换不同技能(比如先做数学推导再做规划),但现有基准大多只测单一技能,无法衡量模型"切换技能"的能力。方法:作者提出"技能熵(Skill Entropy)"来衡量切换难度,构建了覆盖 558 种技能、9 个领域、三档难度的 Skill²-Bench,并把技能熵转化为强化学习训练信号(Skill-Entropy RL),在奖励每步答案正确性的同时,也奖励模型预测出的技能序列与标准答案技能序列的一致性。结果:对 12 个模型的评测显示,在高熵任务上普遍存在明显的"技能切换差距";应用 Skill-Entropy RL 后,Qwen3-4B-Instruct 的分数从 34.4% 提升到 68.4%,Qwen3-1.7B 从 14.6% 提升到 40.1%,超过了同类基线方法。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05139v1

Paper5 | Teaching Nemotron Greek: Mining a Corpus, Adapting Retrieval, and Grounding Generation for Modern Greek across Specialist Domains · 2026-08-06 01:56 北京时间
Problem: Modern Greek is missing from NVIDIA's Nemotron retrieval models and major multilingual retrieval benchmarks, despite mattering for RAG in legal, energy, financial and medical use cases. Approach: the authors adapt the full Nemotron retrieval stack for Greek — corpus mining, synthetic supervision, retriever training, reranker adaptation, and reader fine-tuning — and release a new benchmark, HERA. Result: a plain BM25 baseline beats several off-the-shelf multilingual dense retrievers on specialist Greek corpora; after fine-tuning on ~65.8K Greek pairs a Nemotron 1B embedder lifts nDCG@10 from 0.362 to 0.835, and a LoRA-tuned 30B-A3B reader raises judged answer correctness from 29.4% to 66.9%.
问题:现代希腊语既不在 NVIDIA Nemotron 检索模型的覆盖范围内,也缺席主流多语言检索基准,而它在法律、能源、金融、医疗等领域的 RAG 应用中其实很重要。方法:作者对 Nemotron 检索技术栈做了完整的希腊语适配——语料挖掘、合成监督、检索模型训练、重排序器适配和阅读器微调,并发布了新基准 HERA。结果:在专业领域的希腊语语料上,朴素的 BM25 基线反而优于多个现成的多语言稠密检索模型;在约 6.58 万条希腊语检索数据上微调后,Nemotron 1B 嵌入模型的 nDCG@10 从 0.362 提升到 0.835;经 LoRA 微调的 30B-A3B 阅读器模型,回答正确率评分从 29.4% 提升到 66.9%。

Paper6 | The Loss Does Not See the Basis, but Adam Does · 2026-08-06 01:56 北京时间
Problem: gradient descent on a factored model (W=UV^T) is implicitly biased toward low-rank solutions, but Adam starting from the same initialization is not — and it wasn't clear why. Approach: the authors trace the gap to the loss's gauge symmetry and show the low-rank mechanism only transfers to optimizers that are "gauge-equivariant" (true for GD, momentum, Muon, Shampoo, but not Adam/RMSProp), then test nine update rules on matrix sensing and transformer training. Result: a one-parameter family from coordinate-wise to shared-scalar preconditioning restores the low-rank bias monotonically; in transformers, Adam measurably separates two gauge-equivalent initializations, and on two hyperspectral datasets gradient descent cuts held-out error by 43-44% at the lowest sampling density versus Adam-style updates.
问题:在分解模型(W=UV^T)上做梯度下降会隐式偏向低秩解,但从相同初始化出发的 Adam 却没有这种偏向——原因此前并不清楚。方法:作者将这一差异归因于损失函数的"规范对称性",证明只有"规范等变"的优化器才能继承这种低秩机制(梯度下降、动量法、Muon、Shampoo 满足,Adam、RMSProp 等逐坐标方法不满足),并在矩阵感知任务和 Transformer 训练上测试了九种更新规则。结果:从逐坐标到共享标量预条件的一族过渡方法,能单调地恢复低秩偏向;在 Transformer 中,Adam 会在第一步就明显区分开两个规范等价的初始化;在两个高光谱数据集上,梯度下降在最低采样密度下比 Adam 类方法降低了 43%-44% 的留出误差。
