## Papers

**Paper1 | Don't Drop the BATON: Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation via Agentic Subtask Exploration and Transition-aware Memory — 2026-08-18 01:59 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16889v1

Problem: Long-horizon robot manipulation with VLA+LLM-agent pipelines breaks down because exploring a K-stage task whole costs roughly T^K and failures can't be attributed to a single stage, and subtasks hand off states their successors can't use.
Approach: BATON makes each subtask the unit of exploration (cost becomes additive, T×K), stores solutions in a transition-aware memory, and uses a verifier agent plus handoff/lookahead transitions to keep state compatible across subtasks — no model parameters are updated.
Result: On the RoboMemArena benchmark, BATON improves task success by 11.6% and cumulative success by 14.9% over the prior state of the art.

问题:VLA + LLM 智能体驱动的长时程机器人操作容易失效,因为对 K 段任务整体探索的代价约为 T^K,失败时也无法定位具体是哪一段出了问题,而且子任务之间交接的状态经常不兼容。
方法:BATON 把每个子任务作为独立的探索单元(代价降为线性的 T×K),将方案存入具备"交接感知"能力的记忆库,并用验证智能体加交接/前瞻机制保证跨子任务状态兼容——全程不更新任何模型参数。
结果:在长时程基准 RoboMemArena 上,BATON 相比现有最优方法任务成功率提升 11.6%,累计成功率提升 14.9%。

**Paper2 | Q-based Variational Inverse Reinforcement Learning — 2026-08-18 01:59 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16888v1

Problem: Hand-specifying human preferences for AI systems is often infeasible, and existing inverse-RL methods don't quantify uncertainty, which matters for safety-critical and active-learning settings.
Approach: QVIRL is a Bayesian IRL method that recovers a posterior over reward functions by learning a variational distribution over optimal Q-values from expert demonstrations.
Result: QVIRL performs strongly across gridworlds, Lunar Lander, the Highway Environment, and two Atari games, and is the first Bayesian IRL method shown to train from raw pixel observations.

问题:为 AI 系统手工设定人类偏好往往不可行,而现有逆强化学习方法缺乏不确定性量化,这在安全关键场景和主动学习中尤为重要。
方法:QVIRL 是一种贝叶斯逆强化学习方法,通过从专家演示中学习最优 Q 值的变分分布来恢复奖励函数的后验分布。
结果:QVIRL 在网格世界、Lunar Lander、Highway 环境和两款 Atari 游戏上表现优异,是首个证明可从原始像素观测训练的贝叶斯逆强化学习方法。

**Paper3 | Improving the matrix multiplication exponent with modern optimization and AlphaEvolve — 2026-08-18 01:59 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16884v1

Problem: The best known upper bound on the matrix multiplication exponent ω comes from an optimization problem inside the "combination loss analysis" refinement of the laser method, and further gains require improving that underlying optimization.

结果:综合方法把 ω 的上界降到 2.371177 以下,优于此前的最好结果 2.371339。

**Paper4 | AutoSR: Automatic Symbolic Regression by Searching Research States — 2026-08-18 01:55 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16876v1

Problem: Symbolic regression tools usually keep only the final formula and its fit score, discarding the reasoning trail needed to judge whether a numerically good expression is actually credible outside the observed data range.
Approach: AutoSR introduces "Research States" pairing each candidate equation with its reasoning, evidence, and independent review, developed by proposer-reviewer agents under Monte Carlo tree search, then synthesized into a final report.
Result: Across nine challenges from two benchmark suites, AutoSR recovers algebraically equivalent relations in every case, including three problems no published system had previously solved.

问题:符号回归工具通常只保留最终公式和拟合分数,丢弃了判断一个数值上"拟合得好"的表达式在观测范围之外是否真正可信所需的推理过程。
方法:AutoSR 引入"研究状态"概念,把每个候选方程与其推理过程、证据和独立评审绑定,由提出者-评审者智能体在蒙特卡洛树搜索下逐步展开,最终综合成一份报告。
结果:在两个基准套件的九个挑战任务上,AutoSR 在全部案例中都恢复出代数等价的关系式,其中三个此前没有任何已发表系统解出过。

**Paper5 | Towards Computational Provenance: Carrying Causal-State Evidence in Generated Text — 2026-08-18 01:50 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16868v1

Problem: A language model's output gives no verifiable evidence about which internal computation actually produced it, making claims about a model's internal reasoning path hard to confirm.
Approach: The authors test whether generated text can carry detectable statistical evidence of a specific, causally-verified internal state, using controlled feed-forward and transformer architectures trained on an arithmetic task with two possible internal paths to the same answer.
Result: Both architectures passed all 128 matched-pair evaluations, with a detector reliably recovering which internal path was used; a separate answer-only transformer experiment found no naturally-learned signal without deliberate intervention.

问题:语言模型的输出本身无法验证究竟是哪种内部计算产生的,这让人难以确认关于模型内部推理路径的说法。
方法:作者研究生成文本能否携带可检测的统计证据来证明某个经过因果验证的内部状态确实发生过,实验基于在算术任务上训练、存在两条可通向同一答案的内部路径的受控前馈网络和 Transformer。
结果:两种架构在全部 128 组配对评测中均通过测试,检测器能可靠还原实际使用的内部路径;但在另一项"仅看答案"的 Transformer 实验中,若无刻意干预则未发现自然习得的类似信号。

**Paper6 | What Do Compliance Detectors Read? An Audit of Activation Probes and Guard Models — 2026-08-18 01:37 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16852v1

Problem: Compliance-monitoring detectors for deployed language models are supposed to check outputs against a specific written rule, but accuracy stays unchanged even when the governing rule is deleted, permuted, or swapped — a failure the authors call "rule blindness," found across every guard and activation probe tested.
Approach: They build a benchmark crossing two rules with two scenarios (so neither alone predicts the label) to confirm the failure, then introduce the Internal Compliance Score (ICS), a training-free activation readout, and audit four deployed guard models plus an 8B zero-shot judge.
Result: Step-by-step reasoning was the only approach that escaped rule blindness; ICS did not beat a pre-registered baseline (a bag-of-words model matched it), though it stayed useful for cheap large-scale auditing until an adaptive white-box attack removed its gains.

问题:面向语言模型的合规监测检测器本应依据具体的书面规则判断输出是否合规,但作者发现,即便删除、打乱或替换所依据的规则,检测准确率也几乎不变——他们称之为"规则盲视",在测试的所有守卫模型和激活探针中都存在。
方法:作者构建了一个"两条规则 × 两个场景"的基准(单独任一因素都无法预测标签)来验证这一失效现象,并提出无需重新训练的"内部合规分数"(ICS)激活读出方法,审计了四个已部署的守卫模型和一个 8B 零样本裁判模型。
结果:唯一能逃脱"规则盲视"的是逐步推理方式;ICS 未能超过预注册的基线(一个词袋模型效果与之相当),但在被自适应白盒攻击抵消收益之前,它仍因低成本、可大规模审计而有用。

**Paper7 | Proteus: Incremental Memory Activation for Long-Context Sequence Modeling — 2026-08-18 01:30 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16844v1

Problem: Memory-based long-context sequence models typically use a static memory, letting early tokens over-occupy capacity and "pollute" the state, leaving too little room and causing interference for later context.
Approach: Proteus introduces incremental memory activation — an early bottleneck forcing stronger compression of history, with capacity progressively unlocked as context grows — and can be dropped into existing memory architectures at no extra cost.
Result: Applied to state-of-the-art models (SWLA, Comba, Titans, Hope-Attention), Proteus gives consistent gains on language modeling, reasoning, and long-context retrieval/understanding, with larger gains at longer context lengths.

问题:基于记忆的长上下文序列模型通常使用静态记忆,导致早期 token 过度占用容量、"污染"记忆状态,给后续上下文留下的空间不足并造成干扰。
方法:Proteus 提出"增量式记忆激活"机制——先设置一个较小的早期瓶颈,迫使模型更充分压缩历史信息,再随上下文增长逐步释放容量——可零成本嵌入现有多种记忆架构。
结果:应用到 SWLA、Comba、Titans、Hope-Attention 等先进模型后,Proteus 在语言建模、推理以及长上下文检索/理解任务上都带来一致提升,且上下文越长收益越大。

**Paper8 | HAF: Adapting Generalist VLAs to Humanoid Whole-Body Loco-manipulation via Hierarchical Action Flow and Spectral Latent RL — 2026-08-18 01:22 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16837v1

Problem: Generalist VLA foundation models don't transfer well to humanoid whole-body loco-manipulation because locomotion, waist posture, and dual-arm control are high-dimensional and interdependent, and offline-trained policies stay suboptimal without risky/expensive full-backbone RL fine-tuning.
Approach: HAF splits action generation into a hierarchical, stage-wise flow-matching generator (HAF-VLA) that preserves cross-stage kinematic dependencies, then adds HAF-Steer, an offline-to-online RL pipeline restricted to a compact latent noise subspace so the large VLA backbone never needs updating.
Result: On seven real-world humanoid loco-manipulation tasks, HAF outperforms single-stage VLA baselines and improves whole-body coordination and task performance.

问题:通用 VLA 基础模型很难直接迁移到人形机器人的全身移动-操作任务上,因为移动、腰部姿态和双臂操控高度耦合且维度高,而离线训练的策略若不做真实世界强化学习微调容易表现欠佳,直接微调整个大模型主干又成本高、风险大。
方法:HAF 把动作生成拆分为分阶段的层次化流匹配生成器(HAF-VLA),保留跨阶段的运动学依赖关系;再叠加 HAF-Steer——一种离线到在线的强化学习流程,把优化限制在紧凑的隐空间噪声子空间内,从而无需更新庞大的 VLA 主干本身。
结果:在七个真实世界人形全身移动-操作任务上,HAF 优于单阶段 VLA 基线,全身协调性和任务表现均有提升。

**Paper9 | Model Hypnosis: Strong control of AI via additive subliminal effects — 2026-08-18 01:20 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16834v1

Finding: AI models across families and scales — including frontier reasoning models — are broadly susceptible to "model hypnosis," where individually weak, seemingly irrelevant prompt cues (like paraphrases or typos) can be systematically combined to strongly steer model behavior, and hypnotic prompts can transfer between models.
Why it matters: Because the effect works through inconspicuous textual choices rather than obvious jailbreak language, it poses a new challenge for AI safety and a significant obstacle for interpretability work.

发现:各家族、各规模的 AI 模型——包括前沿推理模型——都普遍容易受到"模型催眠"影响,即单独看起来微弱、无关紧要的提示线索(如改写措辞或打字错误)经过系统性组合后,能够强力操控模型行为,且这类"催眠"提示可以在不同模型之间迁移。
为什么重要:由于这种效应是通过不起眼的文字选择而非明显的越狱式语言起作用的,它给 AI 安全带来了新的挑战,也给可解释性研究制造了重大障碍。

**Paper10 | Policy Iteration with Human Feedback: Bringing Post-Training RL to In-context Learning — 2026-08-18 01:16 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16831v1

Problem: In-context learning lets a fixed pretrained model adapt via instructions/demonstrations, but there was no systematic way to bring post-training-style RL improvement into that fixed-weight regime.
Approach: Policy Iteration with Human Feedback (PIHF) keeps a pretrained LM as a fixed execution substrate and instead revises a versioned natural-language policy and tool set; an LM critic plus a clinical expert review reasoning/tool-use trajectories to propose revisions, with the expert retaining authority over admission/rollback, validated via Recall@1/@5.
Result: On ultra-rare-disease diagnosis benchmarks, PIHF-derived policies improved Recall@1 across four executors (3B-49B parameters), with gains of 32.7 points for GPT-5.4 and 31.1 points for Qwen3.6-35B.

问题:上下文学习让固定的预训练模型可以通过指令/示例来调整行为,但此前没有系统性方法把"后训练式"的强化学习改进引入这种权重固定的范式。
方法:"基于人类反馈的策略迭代"(PIHF)让预训练语言模型保持权重固定、作为执行底座,转而去修订一份带版本管理的自然语言策略和工具集;由语言模型评审员和临床专家共同审查推理/工具调用轨迹并提出修订建议,专家保留是否采纳/回滚的最终决定权,并用 Recall@1/@5 验证效果。
结果:在超罕见病诊断基准上,PIHF 得到的策略在四个执行模型(参数量 30 亿至 490 亿)上都提升了 Recall@1,其中 GPT-5.4 提升 32.7 个百分点,Qwen3.6-35B 提升 31.1 个百分点。
