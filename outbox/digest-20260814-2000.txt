Paper1 · AutoDesign: Meta-Harness Optimization for Long-Horizon Agentic Design
2026-08-14 01:59 (Beijing) · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13560v1
Problem: Existing agent "harness" systems (the scaffolding around a model) are static and don't accumulate reusable experience.
问题:现有 agent 的"harness"(围绕模型的脚手架系统)是静态的,无法积累可复用的经验。
Approach: A meta-harness optimizer guides a code agent to recursively improve its own harness from rollout feedback, tested on a new paper-to-poster benchmark (PosterBench, 100 papers across 5 disciplines).
方法:一个元框架优化器引导代码 agent 根据 rollout 反馈递归改进自身的 harness,在新的论文转海报基准 PosterBench(100 篇论文、5 个学科)上验证。
Result: AutoDesign scores 78.32 on PosterBench Main Track, beating the closed-source commercial system "Claude Design" by 7.45 points, and lifts average scores across seven agent configurations from 54.99 to 67.39 (+12.4%) — a full autonomous run costs under $3.
结果:AutoDesign 在 PosterBench 主赛道得分 78.32,比闭源商业系统"Claude Design"高 7.45 分;在七种 agent 配置上把平均分从 54.99 提升到 67.39(+12.4%);一次完整自主运行成本不到 3 美元。
Why it matters: A concrete data point on self-improving agent scaffolding outperforming a named commercial competitor — worth watching for anyone building agent infrastructure.
为什么重要:这是"自我改进的 agent 脚手架"跑赢一个具名商业竞品的具体数据点,值得关注 agent 基础设施方向的人留意。

Paper2 · OmniScientist: An Omni-Modal Omni-Discipline AI Scientist
2026-08-14 01:59 (Beijing) · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13558v1
Problem: AI-scientist systems automate research workflows but usually reason only over text/code/precomputed summaries, missing raw spatial, temporal, and cross-channel evidence.
问题:现有"AI 科学家"系统能自动化研究流程,但通常只能处理文本、代码或预计算的摘要,丢失了原始的空间、时间、跨通道证据。
Approach: OmniScientist adds a perception layer plus three autonomous agents (ideation, experiment, writeup) that reason directly over raw multimodal evidence (images, signals, audio, video, 3D structures, etc.), with built-in novelty, statistical-validity, and provenance checks.
方法:OmniScientist 加入感知层和三个自主 agent(选题、实验、写作),直接处理图像、信号、音频、视频、3D 结构等原始多模态证据,并内置新颖性、统计有效性和溯源检查。
Result: Across 36 real-data cases in 5 discipline families, it completes the full raw-data-to-manuscript pipeline every time, scores 6.3 on average, and wins 85% of head-to-head comparisons against a variant that only sees precomputed features.
结果:在 5 个学科、36 个真实数据案例中,系统全部完成从原始数据到成稿的全流程,平均得分 6.3;相比只能看到预计算特征的对照版本,在 85% 的正面比较中胜出。
Why it matters: Reinforces that direct perception of raw evidence — not just text summaries — is becoming a prerequisite for credible "AI scientist" claims.
为什么重要:说明直接感知原始证据(而非仅靠文本摘要)正成为"AI 科学家"是否可信的关键前提。

Paper3 · Vero: Can AI Agents Build Formally Verified Software Repositories?
2026-08-14 01:41 (Beijing) · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13522v1
Problem: AI coding agents give no correctness guarantees, and it's unclear whether they can keep implementation and formal proofs coherent across a whole multi-module repository, not just one function.
问题:AI 编程 agent 无法保证代码正确性,目前也不清楚它们能否在整个多模块代码仓库(而非单个函数)层面保持实现与形式化证明的一致性。
Approach: Vero introduces 43 multi-module benchmark instances (Python, Dafny, Verus, Coq → Lean 4) with curated specs and reference implementations, evaluating joint code-and-proof synthesis at repo scale.
方法:Vero 构建了 43 个多模块基准实例(涉及 Python、Dafny、Verus、Coq,统一转为 Lean 4),配有人工整理的规范和参考实现,评估仓库级别的代码与证明联合生成能力。
Result: The strongest frontier agent fully solves only 27 of 43 instances and closes zero specifications on the hardest repositories.
结果:目前最强的前沿 agent 也只能完全解决 43 个实例中的 27 个,在最难的仓库上一条规范都无法闭合。
Why it matters: A sober benchmark showing repo-scale verified code generation is still an open problem — relevant to anyone betting on "trustworthy AI-written software" timelines.
为什么重要:这个基准清楚显示仓库级别的可验证代码生成仍是未解难题,对判断"可信 AI 写代码"的时间线有参考价值。

为什么重要:证明了在合规数据下训练的小模型也能在特定语言上达到接近前沿的表现,对 AI 投资中"数据合规风险"的讨论有参考价值。

Paper6 · Synthetic Persona Pretraining: Alignment from Token Zero
2026-08-14 01:12 (Beijing) · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13482v1
Problem: Alignment and assistant identity are usually bolted on after pretraining, making values a "thin overlay" that's easier to break.
问题:对齐和助手身份通常是在预训练之后才加上去的,这让价值观成为一层"薄薄的覆盖",更容易被破坏。
Approach: Synthetic Persona Pretraining (SPP) annotates pretraining documents with value-aligned first-person reflections and trains on them from token zero, then binds the resulting persona to the assistant identity in post-training.
方法:Synthetic Persona Pretraining(SPP)在预训练文档上标注符合价值观的第一人称反思内容,从训练一开始就学习这些内容,再在后训练阶段把由此形成的人格绑定到助手身份上。
Result: On models up to 3B parameters / 500B tokens, SPP improves constitution-following and jailbreak robustness and reduces misalignment in out-of-distribution moral dilemmas, without hurting capabilities; the effect grows with pretraining budget and is weaker if applied only late in pretraining.
结果:在最高 3B 参数、500B token 规模的模型上,SPP 提升了宪法遵循度和越狱鲁棒性,减少了分布外道德困境中的失调行为,且不损失能力;这一效果随预训练算力增大而增强,若只在预训练后期引入则效果减弱。
Why it matters: A direct, data-backed argument for doing alignment work earlier and deeper in the training pipeline rather than as a post-hoc patch.
为什么重要:这是一个有数据支撑的直接论据——对齐工作应该更早、更深地嵌入训练流程,而不是事后打补丁。

Paper7 · DARTree: Speculative Diffusion Decoding with Autoregressive Draft Trees
2026-08-14 01:43 (Beijing) · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13524v1
Problem: Diffusion-based speculative decoders speed up LLM inference but their draft distributions aren't properly conditioned on tokens already selected along a draft path, capping speedup.
问题:基于扩散模型的推测解码能加速 LLM 推理,但其草稿分布没有很好地基于草稿路径上已选 token 进行条件化,限制了加速效果。
Approach: DARTree is a training-free method that extends an autoregressive correction head from single draft chains to full draft trees, using single-batch tree expansion plus best-first pruning.
方法:DARTree 是一种无需训练的方法,把自回归校正头从单一草稿链扩展到完整的草稿树,通过单批次树扩展加最佳优先剪枝实现。
Result: Across seven math/code/chat benchmarks it gets the best acceptance length and speedup in all tested configurations, accepting up to 12.97 tokens per round (98.6% more than DFlash, 27.9% more than Domino) and up to 9.73x lossless speedup.
结果:在七个数学/代码/对话基准上,所有测试配置中都取得最佳接受长度和加速比,单轮最多接受 12.97 个 token(比 DFlash 多 98.6%,比 Domino 多 27.9%),最高可实现 9.73 倍的无损加速。
Why it matters: Inference-cost reduction of this magnitude is directly relevant to anyone tracking AI infra/serving-cost economics.
为什么重要:这种量级的推理成本下降,对关注 AI 基础设施和推理服务成本经济学的人直接相关。

为什么重要:这是一个面向高风险领域(临床)的确定性、可审计多智能体编排的具体开源模板,这种模式很可能扩散到其他受监管行业。

Paper10 · Toward a Gricean Retreat: Probing LLMs for Knowledge Boundaries and Referent Specificity
2026-08-14 01:13 (Beijing) · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13484v1
Problem: When asked about things outside their knowledge, LLMs tend to fabricate specific plausible-sounding details instead of retreating to safer, more general claims.

Approach: Using a T-REx-based benchmark varying entity familiarity and referent specificity, the authors probe whether model activations encode knowledge-boundary awareness and anticipate referent specificity before generation.
方法:作者基于 T-REx 构建了一个改变实体熟悉度与指称具体度的基准,探测模型的激活是否编码了知识边界意识,以及是否能在生成前预判指称的具体程度。

Why it matters: A precise mechanistic account of why hallucination persists even when models "know" they don't know — useful for anyone building hallucination-mitigation techniques.
为什么重要:这为"模型明明知道自己不知道,却仍然产生幻觉"提供了精确的机制解释,对做幻觉缓解技术的人有参考价值。
