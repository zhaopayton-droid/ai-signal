**Paper1 | SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments — 2026-08-20 01:58 北京时间**
Problem: Training environments for language agents are typically fixed, so the goal distribution stops scaling with the learner even as it improves. Approach: SPADE has one LLM play both an Environment Designer that writes executable Gym-style training environments and a Reasoning Agent that learns in them, optimizing the designer against the agent's estimated regret so tasks stay at the edge of its ability. Result: At 30B parameters, SPADE beats the strongest fixed-environment baseline by +5.3 points averaged across eight benchmarks, +5.7 on BFCL-v4 multi-turn tool use, and +13.9 on ACEBench-Agent.
问题:面向语言智能体的训练环境通常是固定的,导致任务目标分布无法随智能体能力提升而同步扩展。方法:SPADE 让同一个 LLM 同时扮演"环境设计师"(用 Gym 风格接口编写可执行训练环境)和"推理智能体"(在环境中学习),并根据智能体的估计遗憾值来优化环境设计师,使任务始终贴着智能体能力的边界。结果:在 30B 参数规模下,SPADE 在八个基准上平均领先最强固定环境基线 5.3 分,在 BFCL-v4 多轮工具调用上领先 5.7 分,在 ACEBench-Agent 上领先 13.9 分。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19197v1

**Paper2 | ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning — 2026-08-20 01:55 北京时间**
Problem: Teaching multi-fingered robots long-horizon dexterous tasks from raw visuo-tactile input is hard to learn from scratch, and naive RL fine-tuning destroys useful pretrained skills during transfer. Approach: ADEPT pretrains a dexterous policy on generic object reposing, then post-trains downstream tasks with behavior-cloning distillation, critic warm-up, and conservative on-policy updates, mediated by a joint-space "Geometric Fabric" that keeps the RL policy safely within the robot's kinematics. Result: Distilled perceptive students zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a 23-DoF and a 29-DoF robot hand, solving long-horizon tasks from difficult starting states at human-level speed.
问题:让多指机械手从原始视觉-触觉输入中学习长时程灵巧操作任务很难从零训练,而直接做 RL 微调又会在迁移过程中破坏预训练好的技能。方法:ADEPT 先在通用的"物体重新摆放"任务上预训练一个灵巧操作策略,再用行为克隆蒸馏、critic 预热和保守的在线策略更新做后训练迁移,并通过一个联合空间的"几何织物"机制,把 RL 策略约束在机器人运动学的安全范围内。结果:蒸馏出的感知型学生策略在 23 自由度和 29 自由度两种机械手上实现零样本仿真到实物迁移,能从困难初始状态以接近人类的速度完成长时程任务。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19182v1

**Paper3 | Beyond Teacher Likelihood: Group-Calibrated On-Policy Distillation for Long-Context Reasoning — 2026-08-20 01:54 北京时间**
Problem: On-policy distillation gives dense token-level guidance from a teacher, but on long-context tasks it can favor locally plausible answers that miss distributed evidence, drifting from what task verifiers actually reward as input length grows. Approach: GC-OPD normalizes verifier rewards and trajectory-level distillation scores within each rollout group, uses their signed difference as a teacher-verifier disagreement signal, and redistributes that signal across tokens by their relative distillation advantage. Result: On five long-context benchmarks, GC-OPD lifts Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B averages from 29.08 to 40.47 and 35.12 to 44.65 respectively, beating vanilla on-policy distillation's 39.31 and 43.56.
问题:在线策略蒸馏能提供密集的 token 级教师指导,但在长上下文任务中容易偏向局部看似合理、却遗漏了分散证据的答案,随着输入变长,这种偏差与任务验证器真正奖励的目标会逐渐脱节。方法:GC-OPD 在每个 rollout 组内分别对验证器奖励和轨迹级蒸馏分数做归一化,用二者的带符号差值作为"教师-验证器分歧"信号,再按 token 相对蒸馏优势把该信号重新分配到各个 token 上。结果:在五个长上下文基准上,GC-OPD 把 Qwen3-4B 和 Qwen3-8B 的平均分分别从 29.08、35.12 提升到 40.47、44.65,优于原版在线蒸馏的 39.31 和 43.56。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19181v1

**Paper4 | Finetuning Strategies for Querying Sounds by Vocal Imitation — 2026-08-20 01:51 北京时间**
Problem: Retrieving sound effects by imitating them vocally requires an encoder that can match a human vocal imitation to the target sound despite very different acoustic characteristics. Approach: The winning AES AIMLA 2025 Challenge submission compares two fine-tuning strategies — contrastive learning on a frozen pretrained CED encoder, versus joint contrastive-triplet learning with semi-hard negatives on a MobileNetV3 encoder. Result: The report documents the winning setup's configuration in detail for reproducibility, without a new headline number beyond the challenge win itself.
问题:通过人声模仿来检索音效,需要一个编码器能够把音色特征差异很大的人声模仿与目标音效正确匹配起来。方法:这份 AES AIMLA 2025 挑战赛冠军方案报告,对比了两种微调策略——在冻结的预训练 CED 编码器上做对比学习,以及在 MobileNetV3 编码器上做带半难负样本的联合对比-三元组学习。结果:报告详细记录了获胜方案的具体配置以便复现,除夺冠本身外未给出新的量化指标。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19174v1

**Paper5 | Lévy Attention: Single-Pass Predictive Uncertainty for Continuous-Time Attention — 2026-08-20 01:50 北京时间**
Problem: Deep models for irregularly-sampled time series can answer queries at any timestamp but give no signal for how much to trust each answer. Approach: Lévy Attention replaces softmax with a stochastic integral against an inhomogeneous Poisson random measure, which in expectation reduces to ordinary attention but additionally emits, in closed form and at no extra cost, an "evidence" term and a "disagreement" term that combine into a calibrated uncertainty estimate. Result: The swap costs at most 5.6% accuracy on t-PatchGNN, its free uncertainty signal beats 20-pass Monte Carlo dropout, and a single pass ranks 3,383 unseen patients by trust in 1.4 seconds.
问题:面向不规则采样时间序列的深度模型可以在任意时间戳给出预测,却无法告诉用户这个预测有多可信。方法:Lévy Attention 用一个针对非齐次泊松随机测度的随机积分替代 softmax,期望意义下等价于普通注意力机制,同时以闭式解、零额外成本的方式给出"证据量"与"分歧度"两个量,组合成一个经过校准的不确定性估计。结果:在 t-PatchGNN 上,替换注意力机制带来的精度损失最多为 5.6%;其免费获得的不确定性信号优于 20 次蒙特卡洛 dropout 采样;单次前向传播即可在 1.4 秒内为 3383 名未见过的患者按可信度排序。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19171v1

**Paper6 | Learned, Then Lost: A Measured Single-Example Counterfactual in Pre-training — 2026-08-20 01:46 北京时间**
Problem: A single training example's true contribution to a finished model is normally estimated, not measured, because measuring it requires two full pretraining runs differing in exactly one batch row. Approach: The authors ran that exact counterfactual 24 times, training 32 GPT-2 (124M) models from scratch on OpenWebText, injecting one fixed passage (fluent real, fluent fabricated, or random characters) into a single batch row at step 200 of 9,536 and comparing against an uninjected twin across eight seeds. Result: Fifty steps after injection the model predicts the injected passage measurably better (p < 10⁻⁴), but by the final step that advantage is undetectable, even though weight displacement from the injection is 92% settled by mid-training — the injection relocates the model within its loss basin without moving it out.
问题:单条训练样本对最终模型的真实贡献通常只能靠估计,而非直接测量,因为真正测量需要两次完整预训练,且仅在一个 batch 的某一行数据上有差异。方法:作者真正做了 24 次这样的对照实验,从零训练 32 个 124M 参数的 GPT-2 模型,在 9536 步训练中的第 200 步,把一段固定文本(真实流畅段落、虚构流畅段落或随机字符)注入某一行 batch,并与未注入的对照组在八个随机种子上比较。结果:注入 50 步之后,模型对该段落的预测能力有可测量的提升(p < 10⁻⁴);但到训练结束时这一优势已无法检测,尽管注入带来的权重位移在训练中期就已完成 92%——也就是说注入只是让模型在损失盆地内部"挪了个位置",而没有真正移出盆地。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19168v1

**Paper8 | Interpretable AI predicts a 2026 summer dry anomaly in central China — 2026-08-20 01:43 北京时间**
Problem: Seasonal precipitation is hard to predict directly, even though the atmospheric circulation that drives it is more reliably forecast by dynamical models. Approach: The authors use a deep learning model that translates dynamical circulation forecasts into precipitation estimates, then apply layer-wise relevance propagation (LRP) to identify which inputs actually drive the prediction, followed by perturbation tests to confirm the attribution. Result: Forecasts initialized March–May consistently predict a dry anomaly over central China in summer 2026, and LRP identifies northerly winds tied to central Pacific warming as the dominant driver — removing those LRP-flagged features eliminates the predicted dry anomaly.
问题:季节性降水本身很难直接预测,尽管驱动它的大气环流被动力学模型预测得更加可靠。方法:作者使用一个深度学习模型,把动力学环流预测转化为降水估计,再用逐层相关性传播(LRP)方法识别真正驱动预测结果的输入特征,并通过扰动实验验证这一归因是否成立。结果:3-5 月初始化的预测一致指向 2026 年夏季中国中部会出现干旱异常;LRP 显示与中太平洋增暖相关的偏北风是主导因素——移除这些被 LRP 标记出的特征后,预测中的干旱异常也随之消失。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19163v1

**Paper9 | Beyond the Transcript: Detecting Covert Coordination in Latent Multi-Agent Communication — 2026-08-20 01:43 北京时间**
Problem: LLM agents can coordinate through continuous hidden states invisible to public transcripts, opening a channel for covert harmful collusion that transcript-only monitoring can't see. Approach: Verifiable Latent Alignments (VLA) links each decision's private latent-state record to its public action via a shared event ID, combining representation anomaly detection, counterfactual action-distribution influence, and sparse-autoencoder interpretation into a neutral-only monitor, plus a steering framework for intervention. Result: On a controlled multi-agent auction benchmark, the monitor reaches 0.993 AUROC for homogeneous agent pairs (0.854 heterogeneous), and full white-box steering recovers the honest bid distribution completely while cutting collusive low-bid behavior by 47.3 percentage points.
问题:LLM 智能体可以通过连续隐藏状态进行协调,这类通信在公开文本记录中不可见,给隐蔽的有害共谋留下了监控盲区。方法:Verifiable Latent Alignments(VLA)通过共享事件 ID,把每次决策的私有隐状态记录与其公开行动关联起来,结合表征异常检测、反事实行动分布影响分析和稀疏自编码器可解释性分析,构建一个无需攻击样本训练的监控器,并配套一套用于干预的引导框架。结果:在受控的多智能体拍卖基准上,该监控器对同构智能体对达到 0.993 的 AUROC(异构为 0.854);完全白盒引导能完整恢复诚实出价分布,并将共谋低价行为减少 47.3 个百分点。

**Paper10 | Continuous-Time Reinforcement Learning for Controlled Hawkes Jump-Diffusions — 2026-08-20 01:40 北京时间**
Problem: Controlling systems driven by multivariate Hawkes processes falls outside classical stochastic control theory because the Hawkes intensity's memory makes the problem path-dependent (non-Markovian).
