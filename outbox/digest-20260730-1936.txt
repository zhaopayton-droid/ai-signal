Paper1 | Do You Really Need to Pretrain Q-Functions for Online RL Fine-Tuning? · 2026-07-30 01:59 北京时间
Problem: In value-based RL, pretraining then fine-tuning is standard, but it's unclear whether the Q-function itself needs offline pretraining before online fine-tuning. Approach: The authors show naive Q-function pretraining gives little benefit over random initialization because the pretrained Q-function targets the wrong (pretrained) policy, so they propose Initialization via Policy Ensemble (IPE), which trains multiple diverse policies and pools their rollouts to bootstrap Q-learning online. Result: Across a suite of continuous-control benchmarks, IPE improves fine-tuning performance by an average of 1.26x over naive Q-function pretraining.
问题:在基于价值的强化学习中,"先预训练后微调"已是标准范式,但是否也应该对 Q 函数本身做离线预训练,这一点并不清楚。方法:作者发现朴素的 Q 函数预训练相比随机初始化几乎没有优势,原因是预训练得到的 Q 函数瞄准的是预训练策略本身,而非在线微调最终收敛到的策略;为此他们提出 Initialization via Policy Ensemble(IPE),通过训练多个多样化策略并汇总其轨迹来引导在线 Q 学习。结果:在一系列连续控制基准上,IPE 相比朴素 Q 函数预训练平均带来1.26倍的微调性能提升。

Paper2 | Mental World Modeling · 2026-07-30 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Existing world models predict physical scene evolution but ignore agents' hidden mental states (beliefs, wants, intentions), so they can predict the wrong action even when the physical scene is modeled correctly. Approach: The authors formulate Mental World Modeling (MWM), which maintains a coupled physical-mental world state and simulates how candidate actions update both, instantiated as a training-free baseline called MENTIS. Result: Across 8 modern LLM-based world models tested on a curated dataset of situated decision scenarios (text, image, sounding-video), explicitly modeling mental state proved essential for predicting human decisions, though deeper analysis exposed remaining bottlenecks.
问题:现有世界模型能预测物理场景如何演变,却忽视了 agent 隐藏的心理状态(信念、愿望、意图),因此即便物理场景建模正确,也可能预测出错误的行动。方法:作者提出 Mental World Modeling(MWM)框架,维护一个"物理-心理"耦合的世界状态,并模拟候选行动如何同时更新这两部分,将其实例化为一个无需训练、完全可解释的基线 MENTIS。结果:在覆盖文本、图像与有声视频等情境决策场景的自建数据集上,对8个现代基于 LLM 的世界模型的测试表明,显式建模心理状态对预测人类决策至关重要,不过深入分析也揭示了当前方法仍存在的瓶颈。

Paper3 | From Classification to Regression: Using a Fruitfly to Solve Equations · 2026-07-30 01:58 北京时间
Problem: Complex global surrogate models for nonlinear regression can be costly, and scientific data often occupy limited, recurring regions of input space that global models don't exploit. Approach: Inspired by how fruitflies sense their environment, the authors reframe regression as classification-style similarity matching against a finite library of local patterns, weighting stored responses by similarity to the query. Result: Applied to nonlinear dynamical systems, data-driven regression, and physics-informed learning, the approach reduces computational and memory demands while giving explicit control over the accuracy/storage/inference-cost trade-off.
问题:用于非线性回归的复杂全局代理模型开销较大,而科学数据往往集中在输入空间中有限且重复出现的区域,全局模型并未充分利用这一特点。方法:受果蝇感知环境机制的启发,作者把回归问题重新表述为"分类式"的相似度匹配——用查询点与一个有限的局部模式库做相似度比较,再按相似度加权组合已存储的响应来生成预测。结果:该方法被应用于非线性动力系统、数据驱动回归与物理信息学习等场景,在降低计算与存储开销的同时,还能显式地在精度、存储与推理成本之间做权衡控制。

Paper4 | Can AI agents conduct open-ended AI research? Early evidence from two case studies · 2026-07-30 01:57 北京时间
Problem: Forecasts of explosive AI progress assume AI agents can automate AI research, but existing evaluations either test narrow verifiable tasks (excluding open-ended research) or rely on overstretched, noisy peer review. Approach: The authors introduce "shadow evaluations": an agent takes on the core open-ended research question of a high-quality unpublished paper, with the paper's original human authors grading the output; they ran this on two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 submissions, giving frontier agents six days and thousands of dollars of compute. Result: The agents completed all the engineering without human help but made no substantial progress on the actual research questions, and both papers were unambiguously rejected by their authors; the authors identify five recurring failure modes (poor judgment on the publishable bar, uncreative fixes, weak backtracking, poor resource awareness, instruction drift), reproduced with a second model/scaffold.
问题:关于 AI 将实现爆发式进步的预测,很大程度上建立在"AI agent 能够自动化 AI 研究"这一假设上,但现有评测要么只测试狭窄的、可验证的任务(排除了开放式研究),要么依赖负担过重、结果不稳定的同行评审。方法:作者提出"影子评测(shadow evaluations)":让 agent 接手一篇高质量未发表论文的核心开放式研究问题,再由该论文的原作者对 agent 的产出打分;他们在两篇未发表的 NeurIPS 2026 投稿上做了这一测试,给前沿 agent 六天时间和数千美元算力。结果:agent 在无需人工帮助的情况下完成了全部工程实现,却未能在真正的研究问题上取得实质性进展,两篇论文都被原作者明确判定为不合格;作者总结出五种反复出现的失败模式(对"可发表标准"判断力不足、面对研究设计缺陷时缺乏创造性、难以从死胡同中有效回退、资源意识差、指令漂移),并在第二个模型和脚手架上复现了这些失败。

Paper5 | APEX-Accounting · 2026-07-30 01:56 北京时间
Problem: There's no rigorous benchmark for whether frontier models can do the real work of accountants (reconciling accounts, accruing expenses, posting transactions, producing reports). Approach: Mercor, in partnership with Ramp, built APEX-Accounting, a private 160-task benchmark across 10 "worlds," each with an accounting system plus supporting files, with every task authored and graded by accounting/bookkeeping experts using written rubrics. Result: Across nine frontier models, Claude-Fable-5 (Max) leads with 56.4% Mean Criteria@3, ahead of Muse-Spark-1.1 (xHigh) at 52.6%; no model exceeds 2.6% on the strict Pass^8 metric, and increasing token budget from $1 to $50 raises scores overall but reveals a Simpson's-paradox pattern where, within a fixed budget, models spending more tokens per task score lower.
问题:目前缺乏一套严谨的基准来衡量前沿模型能否胜任会计师的真实工作(对账、费用计提、记账过账、生成报告)。方法:Mercor 与 Ramp 合作构建了 APEX-Accounting,这是一个私有的160任务基准,分布在10个"世界"中,每个世界包含一套会计系统及配套的表格、PDF 等文件,所有任务均由会计/记账专家撰写并按书面评分标准打分。结果:在九个前沿模型中,Claude-Fable-5(Max)以56.4%的 Mean Criteria@3 领先,其次是 Muse-Spark-1.1(xHigh)的52.6%;在更严格的 Pass^8 指标上没有模型超过2.6%;把 token 预算从1美元提高到50美元会整体提升得分,但也揭示出一种"辛普森悖论"式的现象——在给定预算约束下,单个任务花费 token 越多的模型,得分反而越低。

Paper6 | Inverse Learning of Latent Risk-Neutral Densities from Irregular Option Quotes · 2026-07-30 01:56 北京时间
Problem: Accurate option prices don't guarantee accurate recovery of the underlying (latent) risk-neutral density, a distinction that matters for pricing and risk models. Approach: The authors compare a simple two-component lognormal mixture against learned operators (DeepONet, a "quote transformer") on both a controlled synthetic benchmark with known ground-truth densities and a chronological real-market NIFTY options benchmark. Result: The mixture model wins on aggregate price/tail errors in the synthetic benchmark, but DeepONet cuts 1% quantile and variance error by 39.0%/34.6% relative to the mixture and a quote transformer cuts L1 error by 16.4% on a misspecified family; on 524 held-out real NIFTY calls, test-time-adapted DeepONet cuts RMSE by 28.3%, but classical per-expiry mixture/SVI fits remain more accurate overall — supporting target-dependent choice of method rather than one universal winner.
问题:期权定价准确并不意味着能够准确还原其背后的隐含"风险中性密度",而这一区分对定价与风险模型都很重要。方法:作者在一个已知真实密度的可控合成基准和一个只提供市场真实历史价格的印度 NIFTY 期权基准上,对比了一个简单的双成分对数正态混合模型与多个"学习型算子"(DeepONet、一种"报价 Transformer")。结果:在合成基准上,混合模型在综合价格及尾部误差上表现最好;但 DeepONet 相比混合模型把1%分位数误差和方差误差分别降低了39.0%和34.6%,报价 Transformer 在结构错误设定的 Merton 族上把 L1 误差降低了16.4%;在524个留出的真实 NIFTY 看涨期权上,经过测试时自适应的 DeepONet 把 RMSE 降低28.3%,但经典的逐到期日混合/SVI 拟合整体上依然更准——说明方法选择应取决于具体目标,并不存在普适的赢家。

Paper8 | The Social Cost of an AI Teammate: How an Artificial Teammate Reshapes Human-Human Communication in Small-Team Decision-Making · 2026-07-30 01:51 北京时间
Problem: Conversational AI is increasingly framed as a "teammate" rather than a tool, but little is known about how its presence changes communication among the human teammates themselves. Approach: The authors ran a randomized controlled study (16 two-student-plus-AI teams vs. 17 all-human three-person teams) doing a high-stakes moral-dilemma decision task, analyzed via Group Communication Analysis, team surveys, and lexical analysis. Result: The AI teammate was the most talkative and self-cohesive member of every team but contributed the least new/dense information; its presence lowered human-to-human responsivity and social impact, and reduced humans' reported sense of belonging and status, with greater AI dominance linked to feeling less valued — an effect present from the very start of the conversation, not something that builds up over time.
问题:对话式 AI 正越来越多地被定位为"队友"而非工具,但目前对它的存在如何改变人类队友之间的沟通方式知之甚少。方法:作者开展了一项随机对照研究(16支"两名学生+一个AI"的团队 对比 17支全人类三人团队),让各团队完成一项高风险的道德困境决策任务,并通过 Group Communication Analysis、团队问卷和词汇分析进行考察。结果:在每一支团队中,AI 队友都是话最多、"自我关联性"最强的成员,但它贡献的新信息量和信息密度却最低;AI 的存在还降低了人类成员之间的响应度与社交影响力,人类成员报告的归属感和地位感也随之下降,AI 在对话中越强势,学生越觉得自己不被重视——而且这种"社交成本"从对话一开始就存在,并非随时间推移逐渐累积。

Problem: State-of-the-art retrieval models increasingly rely on closed training data, creating a reproducibility gap, especially for how English supervision transfers to multilingual retrieval. Approach: The authors build a fully open recipe, curating 665M English contrastive pretraining pairs and 1.88M fine-tuning pairs with mined hard negatives, training a 149M-parameter dense model (DenseOn) and a ColBERT-style late-interaction model (LateOn), then translating the validated data into eight languages to train 307M-parameter multilingual versions on mmBERT-base. Paper10 | Partner Capability Estimation for Task-Agnostic Adaptation in Ad-Hoc Teamwork · 2026-07-30 01:50 北京时间
Problem: Ad-hoc teamwork approaches usually assume a single fixed task and known partner capabilities, but in reality a partner's true capabilities are often hidden and humans may act suboptimally when multiple valid strategies exist. Approach: The authors extend ad-hoc teamwork to a multi-task setting via CE-CM (Capability Estimation via Contextual Models), an approximate Bayesian method inferring task-invariant capability vectors through simulation-based sampling with no population pre-training, plus CE-CM-Div, which evaluates capability hypotheses against diverse planner rollouts to account for human unpredictability. Paper12 | When Do Learned Diffusion Proposals Help Constraint Solving? A Controlled Study on Continuous Algebraic Systems · 2026-07-30 01:44 北京时间
Problem: Solving continuous algebraic constraint systems requires both finding satisfying values and choosing a structural fix when a system is unsolvable; classical solvers only handle the first well, and prior evaluations of learned diffusion-based solvers rarely control for simple random multi-start search under the same budget. Approach: The authors build MARC, which represents the system as a factor graph, uses a graph-neural diffusion denoiser to propose value assignments, refines them via computer-algebra energy descent, and certifies solutions symbolically; they run controlled comparisons against random multi-start on synthetic families and eight real-world systems (robotics, positioning, optimization, algebra). Problem: Existing image-quality-assessment methods target natural photos or AI-generated content and don't transfer to scientific figures, while prior scholarly-chart studies only do visual-surface comparisons without checking caption alignment, citation relevance, or misleading risk. Approach: The authors build SciFigQual-Bench, a full-text-contextual benchmark covering top CS conferences from 2020-2025 with 6,308 images independently scored by multiple domain experts across five dimensions, binding each image to its caption, citing sentence, and manuscript context; they also design SFQ-Agent, a staged cross-modal evaluation framework. Result: On the eval1200 test subset, SFQ-Agent equipped with GPT-5.6-Sol achieves the lowest overall average absolute error (0.418) and highest consistency rate (93.4%), outperforming both direct evaluation and auxiliary vision-language-model evaluation schemes.
