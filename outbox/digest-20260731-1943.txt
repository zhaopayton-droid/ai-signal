Paper1 | Learning to Trace Seiberg Dualities · 2026-07-31 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Determining whether two supersymmetric quiver gauge theories are dual is computationally hard even when the rules are known. Approach: The authors train transformer and MLP networks to learn quiver mutations that trace Seiberg dualities, combined with classical pathfinder search. Result: For quivers with ~10 nodes, the learned models outperform deterministic algorithms, with pathfinder search adding further gains; the authors propose this as a benchmark for frontier AI applied to theoretical physics.
问题:判断两个超对称箭图规范理论是否互为对偶,即使规则已知,在计算上仍然很困难。方法:作者训练 Transformer 和 MLP 网络学习追踪 Seiberg 对偶所需的箭图突变,并叠加经典寻路搜索算法。结果:对约10个节点规模的箭图,学习模型优于确定性算法,叠加寻路搜索后进一步提升;作者提议将其作为前沿 AI 应用于理论物理的基准。

Paper2 | ReToken: One Token to Improve Vision-Language Models for Visual Retrieval · 2026-07-31 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Long visual context degrades vision-language model performance as distractors grow, and processing all visual tokens is infeasible under GPU memory limits. Approach: ReToken adds one learnable embedding, trained as an explicit retrieval target, that selects a sparse set of query-relevant tokens from a pre-filled visual KV cache, trained on only a small image-QA dataset. Result: It improves Qwen3VL-8B by 13.4 points and InternVL3.5 by 12.4 points on Visual Haystacks, and transfers zero-shot to long video for an 8.0-point gain on LVBench, with training and long-video inference both fitting on one H100.
问题:视觉上下文过长会让视觉语言模型性能随干扰项增多而下降,而一次性处理全部视觉 token 在显存限制下并不可行。方法:ReToken 引入一个可学习的嵌入向量作为显式检索目标,从预填充的视觉 KV 缓存中挑出一小部分与查询相关的 token,仅用小规模图像问答数据集训练。结果:在 Visual Haystacks 上让 Qwen3VL-8B 提升13.4分、InternVL3.5 提升12.4分;在 LVBench 上零样本迁移到长视频,提升8.0分,训练与长视频推理均可在单张 H100 上完成。

Paper7 | KAISEN: Reproducible Subgroup Fairness Auditing for Clinical Risk Models · 2026-07-31 01:57 北京时间
Problem: Clinical risk models can look strong on aggregate while producing very different error rates across patient subgroups, and the audit pipelines meant to catch this are rarely stress-tested. Approach: KAISEN is a five-phase fairness-audit pipeline (stratification, disparity measurement, mechanism diagnostics, mitigation, drift monitoring), stress-tested to failure on a synthetic benchmark of 16 disease tasks and 15 social-determinant axes. Result: Per-group threshold optimization reliably reduces disparity while a common calibration fix behaves like a coin flip; a widely-used mechanism diagnostic misses nearly all disparity sources under proxy misspecification; and drift thresholds tuned on one cohort fail to transfer to another.
问题:临床风险模型整体指标可能表现优异,但不同患者亚组间错误率差异很大,而用于发现这类问题的审计流程本身却很少被严格测试。方法:KAISEN 是五阶段公平性审计流程(分层、差异度量、机制诊断、事后缓解、漂移监测),在覆盖16种疾病和15个社会决定因素维度的合成基准上测到失效为止。结果:分亚组优化阈值能可靠降低差异,但一种常用校准方法效果近似抛硬币;某广泛使用的机制诊断工具在代理变量设定有误时几乎测不出模型驱动的差异;在一个队列上调好的漂移阈值换到另一队列就会失效。

Paper10 | VAD: Attributing Visual Evidence for Target Reconstruction in Multimodal On-Policy Distillation · 2026-07-31 01:43 北京时间
Problem: In multimodal on-policy distillation, a teacher's corrections mix visual signal with linguistic priors and teacher-specific effects, making it unclear which corrections are actually supported by visual evidence. Approach: VAD is a counterfactual method that evaluates the same teacher with relevant visual evidence present vs. removed, uses the resulting log-probability shift as a proxy for the visually attributable correction, and reconstructs a student-anchored training target from it. Paper11 | MixFrag: Fragility-Guided Mixed-Precision Post-Training Quantization for Vision Transformers · 2026-07-31 01:43 北京时间
Problem: Post-training quantization for Vision Transformers typically uses uniform bit-widths across components, ignoring differing sensitivity to quantization. Approach: MixFrag estimates per-component "quantization fragility" via KL divergence between full-precision and isolated quantized outputs on a small calibration set, then allocates bit-widths as a Multiple-Choice Knapsack Problem under a target bit budget. Result: On ImageNet-1K it's competitive, and on COCO detection/segmentation it sets a new state of the art among mixed-precision PTQ methods, improving the prior best by up to 9.6 AP under the challenging MP3/MP3 setting.
问题:视觉 Transformer 的训练后量化通常对各组件采用统一比特宽度,忽视了它们对量化的敏感度差异。方法:MixFrag 在小规模校准集上用 KL 散度估计每个组件的"量化脆弱性",再把比特分配建模为多选背包问题,在给定预算下逐层自适应分配精度。结果:在 ImageNet-1K 分类任务上具有竞争力;在 COCO 检测/分割上取得混合精度 PTQ 方法的新最优结果,在较严苛的 MP3/MP3 设置下比此前最优方法提升最多9.6 AP。
