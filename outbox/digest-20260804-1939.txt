Paper1 | AURORA-LM: Autoencoding Unified Representation for Continuous-Latent Diffusion Language Modeling · 2026-08-04 01:59 北京时间
问题:文本生成仍主要依赖离散 token,而图像/视频/音频已转向连续潜空间;现有连续语言模型要么用不适合生成的嵌入空间,要么压缩潜表示牺牲 token 级保真度。方法:AURORA-LM 用 Query 式编码器-解码器构造高容量、可解码的文本潜序列,再用分块因果扩散 Transformer 通过 flow matching 学习其分布,按块从左到右生成、块内并行去噪。结果:在 OpenWebText 自由生成和 XSum 摘要任务上,AURORA-LM 在同类连续/扩散语言模型中表现最强,扩大到 10 亿参数后超过更大的公开潜扩散语言模型(实验全部在华为昇腾 NPU 上完成)。
Problem: text generation still leans on discrete tokens while images/video/audio have moved to continuous latents; existing continuous LMs either use embeddings unsuited for generation or compress latents at the cost of token-level fidelity. Approach: a Query-based encoder-decoder builds a high-capacity, decodable text latent sequence, and a block-causal Diffusion Transformer learns its distribution via flow matching, generating block-by-block left to right with parallel denoising within each block. Result: AURORA-LM is the strongest evaluated continuous/diffusion LM on OpenWebText free generation and XSum summarization, and at 1B params it beats a larger publicly released latent-diffusion LM (all runs on Huawei Ascend NPUs).

Paper5 | UEmbed: Unified Sparse and Dense Multimodal Embeddings · 2026-08-04 01:54 北京时间
