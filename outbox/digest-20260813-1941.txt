**Paper2 | Convergent Detour Hijacking: Task-Preserving Resource Amplification in Skill-Based LLM Agents**
2026-08-13 01:12 北京时间 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12273v1
Problem: LLM agents that use third-party "skills" expose two control points to untrusted publishers — the description used for skill selection, and the instruction body used for planning — and prior work studied selection manipulation and resource-amplification attacks separately, leaving their combined effect unclear.
问题:使用第三方「技能」的 LLM 智能体,会向不受信任的发布者暴露两个控制点——用于「技能选择」的描述文字,以及用于「规划」的指令正文——此前的研究分别单独考察了「选择操纵」和「资源放大」攻击,二者组合起来的效果此前并不清楚。
Approach: The authors introduce Convergent Detour Hijacking (CDH), a text-only attack: a malicious skill's description earns selection, and its instruction body fabricates plausible dependencies during planning, routing the agent through an unnecessary detour via other skills before it rejoins the original task — tested across multiple LLM backends on 491 held-out tasks.
方法:作者提出「收敛式绕路劫持」(CDH)攻击,纯文本、无需依赖运行时:恶意技能的描述文字确保自己在选择阶段被选中,其指令正文再在规划阶段编造看似合理的依赖关系,把智能体引导去调用其他非必要技能绕一圈弯路,之后再回到原任务——在多个 LLM 后端、491 个留出任务上进行了测试。
Result: On DeepSeek-V4-Pro, the attacker's coordinator skill gets selected in 80.02% of tasks; when it does, token consumption rises 66.91% and execution time rises 92.45%, while the task still appears to complete normally — meaning correct output doesn't guarantee a safe or cost-controlled execution trajectory.
结果:在 DeepSeek-V4-Pro 上,攻击者植入的协调技能有 80.02% 的概率被选中;一旦被选中,token 消耗上升 66.91%,执行时间上升 92.45%,而任务表面上仍能正常完成——说明「输出结果正确」并不能保证执行轨迹本身是安全、可控成本的。

**Paper3 | Information Abundance Paradox: Long-Context Training Undermines Parametric Knowledge**
2026-08-13 00:13 北京时间 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12218v1
Problem: Long-context training is being scaled up on the assumption that more context only helps by exposing richer evidence, but whether it changes how models actually learn has gone untested.
问题:业界不断扩大长上下文训练的规模,默认「更多上下文只会带来更丰富的证据、只会有好处」,但这是否会改变模型的学习方式,此前并未得到检验。
Approach: The authors propose the "Information Abundance Paradox" — abundant relevant context reduces the incentive to encode information parametrically — and test it across pretraining on long documents and supervised fine-tuning, tracing the effect to a shift in gradient pressure from feed-forward layers (parametric memory) toward attention (context reliance).
方法:作者提出「信息丰裕悖论」——上下文中相关信息越充裕,模型把信息编码进参数的动力就越弱——并在长文档预训练和监督微调两个场景下进行验证,发现其机制根源在于梯度压力从前馈层(对应参数化记忆)转移到了注意力层(对应上下文依赖)。
Result: In pretraining, widening the context window helps language modeling and closed-book QA only up to an intermediate point, after which performance consistently declines; in fine-tuning, more task-relevant training context helps when context is present at test time but hurts robustness when context is absent or misleading — scaling context alone isn't a free win, even with high-quality long-context data.
结果:在预训练阶段,扩大上下文窗口只能在某个中间最优点之前提升语言建模和闭卷问答表现,超过这个点后性能会持续下降;在微调阶段,训练时提供更多任务相关上下文,能在测试时也有上下文的情况下带来提升,但当测试时上下文缺失或具有误导性时,反而会削弱模型的鲁棒性——即便长上下文数据质量很高,单纯扩大上下文规模也并非「稳赚不赔」。

**Paper5 | AI4AI at Test-Time: Strong-to-Weak Capability Transfer via Harnesses**
2026-08-13 01:53 北京时间 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12307v1
Problem: Most capability transfer from strong to weak models happens through training-time distillation (updating the weaker model's parameters); the authors ask whether transfer can instead happen purely at test time.
问题:目前从强模型到弱模型的能力迁移大多依赖训练时蒸馏(更新弱模型的参数);作者反问:这种迁移能否完全在测试阶段完成,而不更新任何参数?
Approach: A stronger "builder" model iteratively constructs an inference-time harness for a weaker "target" model, refining it over multiple rounds using 5% of the data as a validation set, then evaluating the finalized harness on four Theory-of-Mind benchmarks — with no parameter updates involved.
方法:让一个更强的「构建者」模型迭代地为较弱的「目标」模型搭建推理时的脚手架(harness),用 5% 的数据作为验证集反复打磨,最终在四个「心智理论」基准上评估定型后的脚手架——全程不更新任何模型参数。
Result: This test-time transfer nearly doubles average target-model performance (from 0.49 to 0.91), with gains coming mainly from offloading unstable reasoning into deterministic code, benchmark-specific routing, and strict answer-format enforcement rather than from more extensive reasoning or sampling; weaker targets benefit the most.
结果:这种测试时迁移几乎让目标模型的平均表现翻倍(从 0.49 提升到 0.91),提升主要来自把不稳定的推理过程转移到确定性代码、针对具体基准的路由逻辑,以及严格的答案格式约束,而非让模型进行更多推理或采样;越弱的目标模型,获益越大。

Approach: The authors propose a three-step process: distill a natural-language task into fundamental objectives and measurable outcome variables, select a causally representative subset of those variables as reward terms (formalized as minimum-cost partial cover on a causal DAG, solved via max-flow), and fit term weights through preference elicitation framed as a convex feasibility problem.

Result: The method is, to the authors' knowledge, the first reward-design approach that maintains a deterministically conflict-free feasible weight region, narrowable to a desired tolerance with O(n log κ) preference queries — a formal, non-expert-usable path to human-aligned reward functions.

**Paper8 | AVA-Encoder: Towards Agent-Native Video Representation Learning**
2026-08-13 01:58 北京时间 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12313v1
Problem: Creative AI agents lack an effective way to learn from high-quality human films, because there's no video representation that's both faithful to film content and usable for agentic reasoning and editing.
问题:创作类 AI 智能体缺乏从高质量真人电影中学习的有效方式,因为目前没有一种视频表示方法,既能忠实还原影片内容,又能被智能体用于推理和编辑。
Approach: AVA-Encoder converts video into a knowledge-graph representation (hierarchy/state nodes hold structured text, a linked asset layer holds generated images/audio/video, typed edges preserve relations) and reconstructs video from it, using reconstruction-difference feedback as natural-language "textual gradients" to optimize the encoding policy both during pseudo-training and at test time.
方法:AVA-Encoder 把视频转换为知识图谱表示(层级与状态节点存储结构化文本,关联的资产层存放生成的图像/音频/视频,带类型的边保留各元素间的关系),再从该表示重建回视频;并用「重建差异」反馈作为自然语言形式的「文本梯度」,在伪训练阶段和测试阶段都用于优化编码策略。
Result: AVA-Encoder beats the strongest external baseline by 20.7 percentage points, and in a controlled policy-only setting its pseudo-trained encoder policy outperforms a carefully human-tuned one while using 74.3% fewer system-prompt tokens; the team also releases the framework, a reconstruction benchmark, and the first dataset of high-quality film knowledge-graph representations.
结果:AVA-Encoder 比最强的外部基线高出 20.7 个百分点;在受控的「仅策略」对比中,其伪训练出的编码策略优于精心人工调优的策略,且系统提示词的 token 用量减少了 74.3%;团队还公开了完整框架、一个可靠的重建基准,以及首个高质量电影知识图谱数据集。
