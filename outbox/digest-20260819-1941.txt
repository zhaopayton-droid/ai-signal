**Paper1 | On the Fragility of Self-Improving Agents: Variance, Task Order, and Underspecification — 2026-08-19 01:55 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18066v1

Problem: Memory-based self-improving agents (which learn from a stream of tasks by maintaining a textual memory bank) are promoted as a promising direction, but their reliability had not been rigorously stress-tested.
问题:基于记忆的自我改进型智能体(通过维护文本记忆库、从任务流中持续学习)被视为很有前景的方向,但此前很少有人严格测试过它们的可靠性。
Approach: The authors re-evaluate two memory-based methods across multiple runs (to measure variance) and with randomly shuffled task orders (to test order-dependence), then examine agent memories to probe whether task/environment underspecification explains the fragility, testing this by adding detailed rubrics and environment feedback.
方法:作者对两种基于记忆的方法进行了多次重复实验(以衡量方差),并对任务顺序做随机打乱(以检验对顺序的依赖性),随后检查智能体的记忆内容,验证"任务/环境描述不充分"是否是脆弱性的根源——具体做法是在记忆构建中加入更详细的评分细则和环境反馈。
Result: Agent evaluation is inherently noisy and self-improvement loops amplify that noise; performance is highly order-dependent because prior default task orderings act as a hidden curriculum; adding better specification partially closes the gap but significant fragility remains unexplained.
结果:智能体评估本身就存在较大噪声,而自我改进循环会进一步放大这种噪声;智能体的表现高度依赖任务顺序,因为此前默认的任务排列实际上充当了一种隐藏的"课程安排";加入更充分的任务说明能部分缓解性能下降,但仍有相当一部分脆弱性无法解释。

**Paper2 | TokEval: A Tokenizer Evaluation Suite — 2026-08-19 01:52 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18062v1

Problem: LLM tokenizers are usually chosen with minimal evaluation even though their design directly affects downstream model capability, partly because it's unclear which tokenizer properties actually predict which downstream abilities.
问题:LLM 分词器的选择通常缺乏严格评估,尽管其设计会直接影响模型下游能力——部分原因是目前尚不清楚哪些分词器内在属性能够预测哪些具体的下游能力。
Approach: TokEval introduces evaluation metrics beyond fertility/compression rate — e.g. UTF-8 boundary integrity, digit place-value alignment for math — and validates them via controlled pretraining runs that vary only tokenizer training data, pretokenization, and training algorithm.
方法:TokEval 提出了超越"fertility(分词率)"与压缩率的新评估指标——例如 UTF-8 字符边界完整性、面向数学的数字位值对齐等——并通过只改变分词器训练数据、预分词策略与训练算法的受控预训练实验来验证这些指标的有效性。
Result: Information-theoretic metrics predict language-modeling ability well (Spearman rho up to 0.80), while structure-sensitive metrics (digit/line-break handling) correlate with task accuracy instead — suggesting intrinsic tokenizer measurement could replace some pretraining sweeps.
结果:信息论类指标能较好地预测语言建模能力(斯皮尔曼相关系数最高达 0.80),而结构敏感型指标(如数字、换行处理)则与任务准确率相关性更强——这说明分词器的内在度量指标在某些情况下可以替代成本高昂的预训练扫描实验。

**Paper3 | StagedWorkspace: A Versioned Workspace for Knowledge-Work Agents — 2026-08-19 01:44 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18050v1

Problem: AI agents doing knowledge work (editing docs, spreadsheets, slides, code) often operate on parsed views, native files, review diffs, and submitted artifacts that silently drift out of sync with each other, unlike coding agents which have explicit repository contracts for this.
问题:从事知识工作的 AI 智能体(编辑文档、表格、幻灯片、代码等)所依赖的"解析视图""原生文件""审阅差异""提交产物"之间常常悄悄不同步——而编程智能体已经有明确的仓库(repository)契约来处理这个问题,其他知识工作场景却缺少类似机制。
Approach: StagedWorkspace introduces a versioned workspace that binds parsed records and review diffs to content hashes of the native files as they change, tested via fixed-harness ablations on the OfficeQA Pro and APEX-Agents benchmarks.
方法:StagedWorkspace 提出一个"带版本的工作区",将解析记录与审阅差异同原生文件变化时的内容哈希绑定在一起,并在固定执行框架下用 OfficeQA Pro 和 APEX-Agents 两个基准做消融实验验证。
Result: Dual parsed/native access improves OfficeQA Pass@1 by 8.3-12.1 points and APEX mean rubric score by 4.7-9.2 points over a single-view baseline; the resulting SW-Agent scores 63.9% (Gemini 3.1 Pro) on OfficeQA and 42.1 (GPT-5.4 Nano) on APEX, versus published same-model scores of 29.3% and 25.5.
结果:相比单一视图基线,"解析视图+原生文件"双重访问让 OfficeQA 的 Pass@1 提升 8.3-12.1 个百分点,APEX 平均评分提升 4.7-9.2 分;由此得到的 SW-Agent 在 OfficeQA 上(Gemini 3.1 Pro)得分 63.9%,在 APEX 上(GPT-5.4 Nano)得分 42.1,而此前公开报道的同模型分数分别只有 29.3% 和 25.5。

**Paper4 | Chain-of-Experience for Continual LLM Improvement — 2026-08-19 01:22 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18027v1

Problem: Standard LLM evaluations are zero-shot and ignore whether a model can improve through iterative, inference-time interaction the way humans learn from experience.
问题:标准的 LLM 评估方式基本都是零样本测试,忽略了模型是否能像人类从经验中学习那样,通过推理阶段的多轮交互不断改进。
Approach: The authors define "Chain-of-Experience" (CoE), where models accumulate experiential traces from self-feedback or environmental signals (correctness, test pass rates) across iterations, tested with 8 LLMs (including GPT-5, Gemini-2.5 Pro, Claude-4.5 Sonnet) on math, coding, and knowledge tasks.
方法:作者提出"经验链"(Chain-of-Experience, CoE)概念,让模型在多轮迭代中通过自我反馈或环境信号(正确性、测试通过率等)不断积累经验轨迹,并在数学、代码和知识类任务上用 8 个 LLM(包括 GPT-5、Gemini-2.5 Pro、Claude-4.5 Sonnet)进行了测试。
Result: Iterative experience consistently beats feedback-free baselines, with self-feedback alone yielding substantial gains, a 5.6% overall improvement and 19% lower API cost across tasks/models, and combining feedback channels (e.g. model + correctness signals) producing further gains.
结果:利用迭代经验的效果持续优于无反馈基线;仅靠模型自我反馈就能带来明显提升,在所有任务和模型上平均提升 5.6%、API 成本降低 19%;同时使用多种反馈通道(如模型反馈加正确性信号)还能带来进一步增益。

**Paper5 | The IOL-AI Challenge: An Open Challenge towards Advancing Linguistic Reasoning — 2026-08-19 01:00 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18011v1

Problem: LLM reasoning is mostly studied in domains with pre-given rules (math, code); linguistic puzzles invert this by requiring the solver to first discover the underlying rule system before reasoning within it.
问题:目前对 LLM 推理能力的研究大多集中在数学、代码这类"规则已知"的领域;而语言学谜题恰好相反——求解者必须先自己发现背后的规则系统,才能在其中进行推理。
Approach: The authors ran the IOL-AI Challenge on unseen 2026 International Linguistics Olympiad problems, judged both automatically and — for the first time — by the official IOL Jury under the same rubric used for human contestants, drawing 731 submissions from 46 teams under a strict compute budget, plus a separate benchmark of 15 unconstrained frontier and open models.
方法:作者在 2026 年国际语言学奥林匹克竞赛(IOL)的未公开赛题上举办了 IOL-AI 挑战赛,评分方式既有自动评测,也首次邀请官方 IOL 评委按照人类选手同样的评分标准打分;比赛在严格算力预算下吸引了 46 支队伍共 731 份提交,另外还单独对 15 个不受算力限制的前沿及开源模型做了基准测试。
Result: Claude Opus 4.8 earned a jury score equivalent to a gold medal among the unconstrained models, while the compute-constrained submitted systems scored in the bottom 5% of contestants; capability wasn't purely scale-driven (some 14B models beat models twice their size), and prior knowledge of a problem language didn't meaningfully help solve the reasoning tasks.
结果:在不受算力限制的模型中,Claude Opus 4.8 拿到了相当于金牌水平的评委评分;而受算力预算限制提交的系统,得分则排在人类选手的后 5%。模型能力并非单纯由参数规模决定——部分 140 亿参数模型的表现超过了体量两倍于它的模型;此外,模型对某种语言的先验知识,对解决语言学推理题本身并没有带来明显帮助。

**Paper6 | Policy-Invariant Reward Shaping from LLM Feedback: A Framework for Hybrid RL Agents — 2026-08-19 00:55 北京时间**
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18008v1

Problem: Combining LLMs with reinforcement learning is increasingly common, but the theoretical soundness of using LLM-derived signals as reward is often left implicit and unverified.
问题:将 LLM 与强化学习结合的做法越来越常见,但把 LLM 输出的信号当作奖励使用,其理论上的合理性往往没有被明确验证过。
Approach: The authors formalize the hybrid LLM-planner + RL-controller setup as a Goal-Augmented Markov Decision Process, using the LLM's per-state progress score as a bounded potential function for reward shaping, and verify the result numerically on a small MDP under four potential configurations (including an adversarial one scaled to 20x the base reward).
方法:作者将"LLM 做规划、RL 做控制"的混合架构形式化为一个"目标增强马尔可夫决策过程",把 LLM 给出的逐状态进度评分作为有界势函数用于奖励塑形,并在一个小型 MDP 上、四种势函数配置(包括一种被放大到基础奖励 20 倍的对抗性配置)下做了数值验证。
Result: When the LLM score is used as a bounded potential function, the resulting reward-shaping term provably preserves the optimal policy set even when the LLM's scores are inaccurate — a stronger guarantee than general LLM-as-reward approaches provide.
结果:当 LLM 评分被用作有界势函数时,由此产生的奖励塑形项可以在理论上证明——即便 LLM 评分不准确,也不会改变最优策略集合,这比一般的"LLM 直接充当奖励"方法能提供更强的保证。

**Paper7 | Traceable Trust for Action-Ready Artificial Intelligence in Bioscience — 2026-08-19 00:44 北京时间**
