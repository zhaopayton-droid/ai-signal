Paper1 | MirrorWorld: Taming Video Diffusion Models for Mirror Reflection Generation · 2026-08-08 01:58 北京时间
Problem: Video diffusion models can synthesize high-fidelity video, but mirror reflections inside generated scenes often show the wrong content or wrong spatial layout because the models aren't designed to link a mirror's content to the surrounding scene. Approach: MirrorWorld adds two components — Semantic Relation Distillation, which transfers "what should be reflected" knowledge from a frozen vision foundation model, and Geometric Transformation Alignment, which learns "how it should be arranged" spatially. Result: On a new benchmark built from four repurposed video-mirror datasets, MirrorWorld improves reflection reconstruction quality over both image-based reflection methods and strong video-inpainting baselines.
问题:视频扩散模型能生成高保真视频,但画面中的镜面反射内容经常出现内容错误或空间布局不一致,因为模型本身并没有专门建模"场景与镜面反射"之间的关系。方法:MirrorWorld 引入两个模块——语义关系蒸馏(从一个冻结的视觉基础模型中迁移"该反射什么内容"的知识),以及几何变换对齐(学习反射内容"该如何排布"的空间变换)。结果:在一个由四个现有视频镜面数据集改造而成的新基准上,MirrorWorld 在反射重建质量上超过了基于图像的反射生成方法和强力的视频修复基线。

Approach: Blast Radius is a predictive memory-management layer that estimates how far an incoming prompt's "reach" extends through context and code, using reversible eviction (archiving dead context verbatim) and a mechanism for burying recurring, low-value transcripts, formalized over what the authors call a "Polish context space."
