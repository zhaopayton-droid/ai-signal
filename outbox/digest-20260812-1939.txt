**Paper1 | Why Does CLAUDE.md Keep Growing? Catastrophic Remembering in Agentic Coding**
2026-08-12 00:01 北京时间 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11095v1
Problem: Agentic coding READMEs like CLAUDE.md grow without bound in real repositories — appending an instruction is cheap, but once its rationale is forgotten, deleting it without risking a regression becomes exponentially costly.
问题:CLAUDE.md 这类智能体编程说明文件在真实仓库中会无限膨胀——追加一条指令很便宜,但一旦忘记它的理由,删除它而不引发回归的成本会呈指数级上升。
Approach: The authors name this "catastrophic remembering" (the inverse of catastrophic forgetting) and analyze 247,694 instruction lifetimes across 1,867 repositories, then test whether adding rationale comments can halt the growth.
方法:作者将这种现象命名为「灾难性记忆」(与灾难性遗忘相对),分析了 1,867 个仓库中 247,694 条指令的生命周期,并测试「为指令附上理由注释」能否遏制膨胀。
Result: Prompts more than tripled over their lifetime (+226%) with older instructions least likely to be deleted; rationale comments removed 99.3% of excess instructions in controlled tests and improved real-world instruction-following by up to 23.1%.
结果:提示词在其生命周期内平均膨胀超过三倍(+226%),且指令越旧越不容易被删除;在受控实验中,附加理由注释能消除 99.3% 的冗余指令,在真实场景中还能将指令遵循度提升多达 23.1%。

**Paper2 | SkillZip: Evaluation-Free Skill Compression for Self-Evolving Agents by Discovering Reusable Structure**
2026-08-11 23:41 北京时间 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11079v1
Problem: Self-evolving agents accumulate skills by appending successful procedures and fixes, so the same rule gets restated across branches and examples, making skills expensive to inject and hard to maintain.
问题:自我进化的智能体通过不断追加成功流程和修复方案来积累技能,导致同一条规则在不同分支和示例中被反复重述,使得技能注入成本高、难以维护。
Approach: SkillZip compresses a skill by finding its shortest faithful structural explanation — stating repeated rules once, factoring shared action sequences, and keeping only genuine exceptions — without needing rollout-based evaluation.
方法:SkillZip 通过寻找技能的「最短忠实结构化表达」来压缩它——将重复规则只陈述一次,把共享的动作序列提取出来,只保留真正的例外情况——且无需依赖基于 rollout 的评估。
Result: The method demonstrates strong compression performance, generalizability, and low cost overhead, with both a one-shot mode and a continual "Zip-on-Write" mode that updates skills without replaying task history.
结果:该方法在压缩效果、泛化能力和成本开销上均表现出色,并提供一次性压缩模式和持续「写入即压缩」模式,后者无需回放历史任务即可更新技能。
