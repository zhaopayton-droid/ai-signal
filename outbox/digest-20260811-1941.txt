Paper1 | Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs · 2026-08-11 01:24 北京时间
Problem: LLM providers hide chain-of-thought as encrypted client-side blocks to protect IP, but the blocks are interchangeable across sessions, users, and even models within a provider. Approach: The authors inject an encrypted reasoning trace from one model into a weaker, less-guarded model from the same provider, forcing it to decode and output the trace in plaintext — demonstrated across Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. Result: This enables reasoning-extraction, and by decoding 315,320 publicly scraped reasoning blocks the authors recovered 367 PII artifacts and 182 credentials; it also enables invisible prompt injection via encrypted blocks. They propose cryptographic and system-level mitigations after responsible disclosure.
问题:LLM 提供商把思维链隐藏为客户端加密数据块以保护知识产权,但这些数据块在同一提供商内跨会话、跨用户甚至跨模型是可互换的。方法:作者把某个模型的加密推理轨迹注入到同一提供商旗下防护更弱的模型中,迫使其解密并以明文输出——已在 Anthropic、OpenAI、Google 三家上验证。结果:这使得推理内容可被窃取,通过解码从公开渠道抓取的 315,320 个推理数据块,作者还原出 367 条 PII 和 182 组凭证;该漏洞还能被用于借加密数据块实现隐形提示注入。作者在负责任披露后提出了密码学和系统层面的缓解方案。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867v1

Paper2 | Multi-Agent AI Safety as an Institutional Design Problem · 2026-08-11 00:47 北京时间
Problem: As AI agents work inside systems governing delegation, information flow, and shared resources, it's unclear which parts of such an "AI institution" actually produce safety. Approach: The first paper from POLIS (a research program on algorithmic institutions) runs a frozen 5,280-episode study suite across four model families, comparing rule formulations and authority-state designs. Result: A detailed constitutional prompt and a provenance-aware executable guard both produce 0/384 violations, but a local-state guard fails specifically when a transformation changes visible policy while origin authority stays fixed (22/96 violations vs. 0/96 for provenance enforcement, p=4.77×10⁻⁷) — showing the same violation rate can hide very different underlying mechanisms.
问题:随着 AI agent 越来越多地在管理任务委派、信息流转和共享资源的系统内运作,目前尚不清楚这类"AI 制度"中究竟是哪些部分真正产生了安全性。方法:POLIS 研究项目(专注于多 agent 系统的算法制度研究)的第一篇论文,在四个模型家族上运行了一套冻结的 5,280 回合研究套件,比较不同的规则表述和权限状态设计。结果:一个详细的宪法式提示词和一个感知来源的可执行守护机制都做到了 0/384 违规,但一个仅依赖本地状态的守护机制在"变换改变了可见策略但原始权限保持不变"的场景下会失效(22/96 违规,而来源溯源机制为 0/96,p=4.77×10⁻⁷)——说明同样的违规率背后可能是截然不同的机制。

Paper3 | SHE: Trajectory-driven Safety Harness Evolution for LLM Agents · 2026-08-11 01:35 北京时间
Problem: LLM agent safety depends on the agent harness (context, memory, tools, permissions), but existing safety mechanisms treat the harness as fixed, unable to evolve with new risks. Approach: SHE decomposes the harness into four artifacts (System Prompt, Rule Bank, Safety Memory, Tool Policy) and runs an attribution-guided evolution loop that turns trajectory failures into structured diagnoses and refines the relevant artifact. Result: On Agent-SafetyBench, SHE achieves a 3.1x attack-success-rate reduction versus a static harness while improving benign utility, and generalizes to unseen risks on the held-out AgentHarm benchmark without further evolution.
问题:LLM agent 的安全性不只取决于模型权重,还取决于管理上下文、记忆、工具和权限的 agent harness,但现有安全机制通常把 harness 当作固定不变的部署产物,无法随新风险演化。方法:SHE 把 harness 拆解为四个组件(系统提示词、规则库、安全记忆、工具策略),并运行一个由归因引导的演化循环,把轨迹中的失败案例转化为结构化诊断,进而改进对应组件。结果:在 Agent-SafetyBench 上,相比静态 harness,SHE 把攻击成功率降低了 3.1 倍,同时提升了正常场景下的可用性,并且无需额外演化即可泛化到未见过的 AgentHarm 基准上的新风险。

Paper4 | Decoding-Level Taboo: A Diagnostic Stress Test for LLM Robustness · 2026-08-11 01:47 北京时间
Problem: Standard LLM evaluations measure performance along a narrow, highly optimized generation path, creating an illusion of capability that diverges from real deployments where guardrails and constraints force models off that path. Approach: "Taboo" is a zero-prompt diagnostic that intervenes directly in logit space, dynamically masking primary candidate tokens at word boundaries to force circumlocution. Result: Across open-weight model families, off-path robustness scales with both parameter count and post-training alignment, and the authors propose Taboo as a general primitive for synthetic data generation and pre-deployment reliability auditing.
问题:标准的 LLM 评测通常只衡量模型在一条狭窄、高度优化的生成路径上的表现,这制造了一种能力假象,与真实部署中受护栏和约束偏离该路径的情形存在落差。方法:"Taboo" 是一种零提示词的诊断方法,直接在 logit 空间进行干预,在词边界处动态屏蔽首选候选 token,迫使模型迂回表达。结果:在多个开放权重模型家族上,偏离路径后的鲁棒性随参数规模和后训练对齐程度提升而增强,作者提出 Taboo 可作为生成合成数据和部署前可靠性审计的通用工具。

Paper5 | Consilience for Verifier-Free Test-Time Scaling · 2026-08-11 01:45 北京时间
Problem: Confidence-based verifier-free test-time scaling (VF-TTS) is attractive for LLM reasoning since it needs no external verifier, but the authors show it catastrophically breaks down on complex tasks — uniformly high confidence often signals a failure to explore rather than a correct answer. Approach: "Consilience" is a selection framework that penalizes high initial confidence while demanding high final confidence, capturing the ideal trajectory of exploratory-then-convergent reasoning. Result: Across graduate-level math and free-form code generation, consilience outperforms existing confidence-based baselines.
问题:基于置信度的无验证器测试时扩展(VF-TTS)因不需要外部验证器而颇具吸引力,但作者发现它在复杂任务上会灾难性失效——均匀偏高的置信度往往意味着模型没有充分探索,而不是答案正确。方法:"Consilience" 是一种选择框架,惩罚初期过高的置信度,同时要求最终置信度足够高,以捕捉"先探索、后收敛"这一理想推理轨迹的特征。结果:在研究生水平数学题和自由形式代码生成任务上,consilience 都优于现有的基于置信度的基线方法。

Paper6 | Mismatch Matters: On-Policy Distillation Beyond Token Agreement (TIDE) · 2026-08-11 00:53 北京时间
Problem: On-policy distillation (OPD), core to modern LLM post-training, has a failure mode called "degenerate agreement" — students exploit repetitive loops to fake near-perfect token agreement with the teacher despite globally flawed responses. Approach: TIDE (Token-level Independent Deficit-Excess correction) separately handles student-excess tokens (bounded Hellinger shaping) and student-deficit tokens (analytic teacher top-K injection) instead of optimizing raw token agreement. Result: Across Qwen3 teacher-student pairs on math reasoning benchmarks, TIDE beats standard OPD and recent baselines, with the largest gains under strong mismatch — improving Avg@8 from 6.9% to 20.3% while cutting response length 3.6x and reducing formatting failures.
问题:在现代 LLM 后训练流程中扮演核心角色的同策略蒸馏(OPD)存在一种"退化式一致"的失效模式——学生模型通过重复循环制造出与教师模型近乎完美的 token 一致性,但整体回答其实是有缺陷的。方法:TIDE(token 级独立的欠缺-过剩修正)分别处理"学生过剩 token"(用有界 Hellinger 整形抑制)和"学生欠缺 token"(用解析式教师 top-K 注入来补足),而不是直接优化原始的 token 一致性。结果:在多组 Qwen3 教师-学生模型对的数学推理基准上,TIDE 优于标准 OPD 和近期基线方法,在教师-学生差异较大时增益最明显——把 Avg@8 从 6.9% 提升到 20.3%,同时把回答长度缩短 3.6 倍并显著减少格式错误。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09836v1

Paper7 | Distill Skills into Weights, Not Prompts (SKALD) · 2026-08-11 00:43 北京时间
Problem: Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards gives no group-relative training signal when rollout groups are uniformly correct or uniformly wrong — 63-68% of groups in the authors' experiments. Approach: SKALD (Skill-Anchored Latent Distillation) self-distills from a "teacher" view of the same model (conditioned on an abstract skill card) into a "student" view (question-only), using an annealed exponentially-tilted objective and a gate that only activates when verified rollouts show positive teacher advantage. Result: Across five held-out math benchmarks, SKALD improves avg@8 over GRPO by +2.46/+4.85/+12.01 at the 0.6B/1.7B/4B scales respectively, remaining ahead of FLOP-matched GRPO.
问题:基于可验证奖励的强化学习,在一组 rollout 全对或全错时(在作者实验中占 63%-68% 的比例)无法提供组内相对训练信号。方法:SKALD(技能锚定潜在蒸馏)让同一个模型以"教师"视角(以一张抽象技能卡为条件)向"学生"视角(仅给问题)进行自蒸馏,使用退火式指数倾斜目标函数,并设置一个只在已验证 rollout 显示教师优势为正时才激活的门控。结果:在五个未参与训练的数学基准上,SKALD 在 0.6B/1.7B/4B 三个规模上相比 GRPO 分别提升 avg@8 达 +2.46/+4.85/+12.01,并持续领先于计算量相当的 GRPO。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09826v1

Paper8 | Fusion Training for Mathematical Generalization in Large Language Models · 2026-08-11 01:41 北京时间
Problem: Thinking Mode Fusion (TMF) lets one LLM support both concise and long-form reasoning by unifying thinking/non-thinking modes, but the training dynamics — data ratio and schedule between modes — remain underexplored. Approach: A systematic study varying thinking-to-non-thinking data ratios and three training schedules, focused on math problem solving. Result: The two modes interact asymmetrically — more non-thinking supervision reduces thinking-mode accuracy — and the optimal training schedule depends on the data ratio, with a quantified negative correlation between the two modes' supervision.
问题:Thinking Mode Fusion(TMF)让单个 LLM 同时支持简洁回答和长篇推理,通过在一个模型内统一"思考"与"非思考"两种模式,但两种模式之间的训练动态(数据配比和训练日程)此前研究不足。方法:围绕数学解题任务,系统性地研究不同的思考/非思考数据配比以及三种训练日程安排的影响。结果:两种模式之间存在非对称的相互作用——增加非思考模式的监督会降低思考模式的准确率,且最优训练日程取决于数据配比,两种模式的监督之间存在可量化的负相关关系。

Paper9 | BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning · 2026-08-11 01:39 北京时间
Problem: Combining in-context learning with reasoning typically requires verbalized intermediate steps, which is costly and may not reflect how the model actually computes. Approach: BDH-CQ solves queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent space, with inference-time inputs continuously updating a recurrent memory, evaluated on the public ARC-AGI-1 set with controlled interventions. Paper10 | Multimodal Model Diffing for Feature Discovery and Control (MMDiff) · 2026-08-11 01:59 北京时间
Problem: Multimodal LLMs show strong visual understanding, but the internal features causing specific behaviors remain hard to identify, audit, or control — standard sparse-autoencoder (SAE) inspection doesn't isolate what multimodal training actually changed. Approach: MMDiff trains multimodal SAEs and diffs a base-LM SAE against its multimodal-adapted counterpart to isolate causal features, then uses per-token contrastive analysis and causal feature removal/steering, tested on LLaVA-MORE, PaliGemma 2, and InternVL3.5. Result: Removing discovered features degrades targeted behaviors by an average of 12% on spatial tasks and 17% on OCR, and cuts multimodal-safety attack success by 24% with no VQA impact; steering the same features improves spatial and OCR accuracy by +3.6%/+1.8%.
问题:多模态 LLM 展现出很强的视觉理解能力,但导致特定行为的内部特征仍难以识别、审计或控制——标准的稀疏自编码器(SAE)检查方法无法准确定位多模态训练究竟改变了哪些特征。方法:MMDiff 训练多模态 SAE,并将基础语言模型的 SAE 与其多模态适配版本做差异对比以定位因果特征,再结合逐 token 对比分析以及对因果特征的移除/引导操作,在 LLaVA-MORE、PaliGemma 2 和 InternVL3.5 上进行测试。结果:移除发现的特征会使目标行为平均下降 12%(空间任务)和 17%(OCR),并把多模态安全攻击的成功率降低 24%,且不影响 VQA 表现;对同样的特征进行引导则能将空间和 OCR 准确率分别提升 +3.6%/+1.8%。

Paper12 | Agentic Harnesses: LLM-Driven Verification Layers for Robot Autonomy · 2026-08-11 01:15 北京时间
Problem: Robot autonomy research has focused on executing plans from LLM-driven planning models, but not on verifying whether proposed actions are actually feasible or safe before execution.
