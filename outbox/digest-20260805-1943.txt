Paper1 | ParVL: Parallel Scaling and Expandable Compute Allocation for Multimodal LLMs · 2026-08-05 01:59 北京时间
Problem: scaling multimodal LLMs by adding parameters or sequential inference compute is costly, and existing methods can't flexibly reallocate compute between the vision (ViT) and language (LLM) components per task. Approach: ParVL reuses the existing ViT/LLM backbone weights across multiple parallel branches via branch-specific prefix parameters, trained end-to-end with full-parameter SFT on roughly 13B tokens. Result: ParVL beats same-recipe single-branch baselines, and the best vision/language compute split varies by task, with no universal ratio.
问题:扩展多模态大模型通常靠堆参数或增加序列推理算力,成本高,且现有方法无法按任务灵活调配 ViT(视觉)和 LLM(语言)之间的算力。方法:ParVL 通过共享 ViT/LLM 主干权重、附加分支专属前缀参数,构建多条并行计算流,并在约 130 亿 token 上做端到端全参数微调。结果:ParVL 优于同配方单分支基线,且最佳视觉/语言算力配比因任务而异,不存在通用比例。

Paper2 | SocietyBench: Forecasting Counterfactual Social-World Evolution · 2026-08-05 01:59 北京时间
Problem: LLM agents are mostly benchmarked on task completion, while their ability to forecast how real social events unfold is barely measured. Approach: SocietyBench turns news/social-media timelines into date-indexed forecasting questions, then anonymizes entities and shifts dates to create counterfactual worlds that block pre-training memorization, scoring on calibration and temporal accuracy. Result: across five events and 125 prediction points, the best of six frontier LLMs scores only 75/100 against a 50-point trivial baseline, agent frameworks don't beat the base model, and calibration and timing accuracy often diverge in the same model.
问题:LLM 智能体的评测大多围绕"任务是否完成",而它们预测真实社会事件走向的能力几乎没被衡量过。方法:SocietyBench 把新闻和社交媒体的时间线转成按日期切片的预测问题,并对实体匿名化、整体平移日期,构造出"结构相同但表面标签被抹去"的反事实世界以防止预训练记忆,分别对"概率校准"和"时间准确性"打分。结果:在 5 个事件、125 个预测点上,六个前沿模型中最强的也只拿到 75/100(基线为 50 分),agent 框架并未超过其基座模型,同一模型在校准和时间准确性上的表现也经常不一致。

Paper3 | WorldCup Arena: Prospective, Leakage-Free Evaluation of Frontier LLMs on a Live Tournament · 2026-08-05 01:59 北京时间
Problem: forecasting benchmarks for LLMs are almost always retrospective, so results have to defend against memorization since the answers already exist on the web. Approach: over the 39 days of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, six frontier LLMs with web search predicted a 7-market card before every kickoff across all 104 matches (4,494 scored predictions total), making leakage impossible by construction. Result: models averaged 63.9% on match outcome (matching "just bet the favorite"), agreed with each other more than they were right, under-called draws, and accuracy tracked how lopsided a matchup was rather than how much information was available.
问题:LLM 的预测类基准几乎都是回顾式的,答案早已在网上,评测必须想办法防止模型靠记忆作弊。方法:在 2026 世界杯 39 天赛程里,六个具备网页搜索能力的前沿模型在每场开球前对全部 104 场比赛填写 7 个市场的预测卡(共 4494 条被打分的预测),因为答案在提问时尚不存在,从结构上就杜绝了数据泄露。结果:模型在胜负预测上平均命中率 63.9%,和"直接压夺冠热门"差不多;模型之间彼此的一致程度高于它们各自的准确率;普遍低估平局概率;准确率更多取决于比赛悬殊程度,而非可获得信息的多少。

Paper4 | TurnSight: Turn-Level Hindsight Self-Distillation for Tool-Integrated Reasoning · 2026-08-05 01:59 北京时间
Problem: RL for tool-integrated reasoning usually relies on trajectory-level supervision, which is too coarse for credit assignment in long, multi-turn tool-use episodes. Approach: TurnSight derives supervision from execution-conditioned hindsight rather than ground-truth answers, builds multiple hindsight views at different lookahead horizons, cross-checks them for agreement, and uses the reliable signal to reweight RL advantages turn by turn. Result: consistent gains across three benchmarks, per the paper's experiments.
问题:面向工具调用推理的强化学习通常只用轨迹级别的监督信号,在长回合、多轮工具调用的场景里很难做到精细的信用分配。方法:TurnSight 不依赖标准答案,而是从"执行后的事后视角"中提取监督信号,构造多个不同前瞻窗口的"事后视图"并互相校验一致性,再用可靠信号按回合调整强化学习的优势值。结果:在三个基准上均取得一致的提升。

Paper5 | PAST-Bench: Benchmarking the Foundations of Recursive Self-Improvement in Personal Agents · 2026-08-05 01:58 北京时间
Problem: personal AI agents accumulate preferences, task history, and skills across sessions, but whether that retained experience actually improves them over time has never been systematically tested. Approach: PAST-Bench runs agents through 26 scenarios and 204 episodes with retained memory toggled on/off, checking both later-task gains and whether the improvement follows the intended save-retrieve-update pathway; the authors also build Hermes+, adding five targeted interventions to the Hermes agent framework. Result: across seven base models and four frameworks, improvement from memory is real but uneven, and agents with the same headline gain can differ a lot in whether that gain is actually backed by correct memory use; Hermes+ raises average gains and gives clearer pathway evidence.
问题:个人 AI 智能体会在多个会话之间积累偏好、任务历史和技能,但这些"留存经验"是否真的能让它们随时间变得更好,此前从未被系统检验过。方法:PAST-Bench 让智能体在 26 个场景、204 个episode 中运行,并开关"是否保留记忆"作为对照,既看后续任务是否有提升,也看这种提升是否真的走了"存储-检索-更新"这条预期路径;作者还基于 Hermes 框架加入五项针对性改造,做出 Hermes+。结果:在七个基座模型和四个 agent 框架上,记忆带来的提升是真实存在但并不均衡的,有些模型即便"提升幅度"看起来一样,背后是否真的用对了记忆机制却相差很大;Hermes+ 提升了平均增益,也提供了更清晰的路径证据。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04003v1

Paper6 | Test-Time Scaling in Reasoning LLMs: Inference Regimes, Evaluation, and Reproducibility · 2026-08-05 01:57 北京时间
Problem: "test-time scaling" now lumps together very different inference algorithms (extending one trajectory, sampling+voting over many, searching partial states) that have different statistics and failure modes, making cross-paper accuracy comparisons unreliable. Approach: the paper formalizes test-time scaling as budgeted inference over an autoregressive model's prefix tree, separates end-to-end system performance from candidate-bank diagnostics, and specifies what's needed for exact vs. distributional reproducibility, releasing 2B+ full reasoning traces. Result: a systematic framework plus a large reproducible trace dataset for benchmarking future test-time-scaling work; no single new "best" method claimed.
问题:"test-time scaling(测试时扩展)"这个词现在被用来指代好几种统计特性和失败模式完全不同的推理算法(延长单条推理链、采样+投票、搜索未完成的中间状态等),导致不同论文之间的准确率没法直接比较。方法:论文把 test-time scaling 形式化为在自回归模型的前缀树上做"有预算的推理",把"整体系统表现"和"候选池诊断指标"区分开,并明确了"精确复现"和"分布级复现"分别需要哪些材料,同时开源了超过 20 亿条完整推理轨迹。结果:提供了一套系统化的评测框架和可复现的大规模数据集,供后续 test-time scaling 研究对齐比较,本文本身不主张某个新的"最优方法"。

Paper7 | Agogic: Performance-Timed Music Tokens for LLM-Native Text-to-Symbolic-Music Generation · 2026-08-05 01:56 北京时间
Problem: text-to-music models pick a music tokenization scheme almost by default, and its actual effect (isolated from model size, data, and training recipe) has never been measured. Approach: holding backbone (Qwen3.5, 0.8B-27B), data, and decoding fixed, the authors swap only the tokenization across seven schemes, including their own performance-resolution scheme PMT (10ms timing, per-note velocity). Result: representation, not model size, is the binding variable — scaling the backbone 34x barely moves generation quality (FMD), while switching to PMT roughly halves it, so a 0.8B PMT model beats a 27B beat-grid model.
问题:文本转音乐模型通常"顺手"就选定了某种音乐 token 化方案,但这个选择本身(剥离掉模型大小、数据、训练配方的影响)的作用从未被单独测量过。方法:作者固定主干模型(Qwen3.5,0.8B-27B)、数据和解码策略,只切换 7 种不同的 token 化方案,其中包括他们自己提出的"演奏级"方案 PMT(10 毫秒级时间精度、逐音符力度)。结果:决定生成质量的关键变量是"表示方式"而非模型规模——主干扩大 34 倍,生成质量指标(FMD)几乎不变,而换成 PMT 表示能让该指标近乎减半,以至于一个 0.8B 的 PMT 模型能打过 27B 的节拍网格模型。

Paper8 | When Attention Goes Blind: Numerical Failure in ALiBi Positional Encodings · 2026-08-05 01:54 北京时间
Problem: ALiBi is a widely used positional encoding, but the authors find its linear bias scaling can underflow floating-point precision, zeroing out a large fraction of attention weights and leaving affected heads "blind." Approach: the authors analyze and characterize the failure, confirm it occurs in state-of-the-art pretrained ALiBi models, run controlled pretraining experiments on 148M-parameter models to isolate the effect, and test four mitigation strategies. Result: the bug can substantially hurt long-context token retrieval while barely affecting standard benchmarks; log-scaled distances give the most consistent fix, though default ALiBi slopes remain a surprisingly strong baseline for needle-in-a-haystack retrieval.
问题:ALiBi 是一种被广泛使用的位置编码方案,但作者发现它的线性偏置缩放会在浮点数精度下发生下溢,导致大量注意力权重被清零,相当于部分注意力头"失明"。方法:作者分析并刻画了这个失效模式,确认它确实存在于已经发布的顶尖 ALiBi 预训练模型中,并用 1.48 亿参数的模型做受控预训练实验来单独分离这一效应,同时测试了四种缓解策略。结果:该 bug 会显著损害长上下文的 token 检索能力,却几乎不影响常规基准表现;对数缩放距离是最稳定的修复方式,不过默认的 ALiBi 斜率在"大海捞针"式检索任务上依然是意外地强的基线。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03994v1

Problem: synthetic pathology images could ease data scarcity in computational pathology, but standard evaluation metrics (FID, Inception Score) rely on ImageNet-pretrained features that may not suit histopathology, so "quality" is poorly measured. Paper11 | Can Large Language Models Recover Semantic Optimization Opportunities That Compilers Miss? · 2026-08-05 01:47 北京时间
Problem: optimizing compilers miss profitable transformations when the semantics that would license them aren't visible in the program representation the compiler analyzes. Approach: the authors introduce SeGaBench, a 120-case executable benchmark (100 synthetic + 20 source-backed) where LLMs must recover hidden semantics from C/C++ context and produce validated, correctness-preserving optimized code, checked against oracle artifacts and performance protocols. Result: the strongest model produces correct artifacts in 94.8% of responses and hits at least 1.05x speedup in 83.3% of cases, though correct fixes often close only part of the gap to the oracle — LLMs look useful as validated "semantic proposers" alongside compilers, not replacements.
问题:优化编译器会漏掉一些本可以做的有效变换,原因是支撑这些变换合法性的语义信息,在编译器分析的程序表示里根本不可见。方法:作者构建了 SeGaBench,一个含 120 个可执行用例(100 个合成 + 20 个真实源码)的基准,要求 LLM 从 C/C++ 上下文中还原出隐藏语义,并生成经过正确性验证、保持程序契约的优化产物,再用 oracle 参考实现和性能协议校验。结果:表现最好的模型有 94.8% 的回复能生成正确产物,83.3% 的用例至少拿到 1.05 倍加速,但正确的修复往往只弥补了与 oracle 之间差距的一部分——结论是 LLM 更适合作为"经过验证的语义提议者"辅助编译器,而非替代编译器。

Paper12 | Video-DeepResearch: Towards the Next-Generation Multimodal Deepresearch Agent · 2026-08-05 01:45 北京时间
Problem: extending multimodal deep-research agents from static images to continuous video requires dense spatiotemporal grounding plus open-web exploration, and current models show "modality bias" (skipping visual tools for text search) and "parametric knowledge leakage" (relying on memorized knowledge instead of actually using tools). Approach: Video-DR uses a decoupled perception-exploration pipeline with stage-wise tool unlocking that forces exhaustive cross-frame visual grounding before web retrieval, trained via SFT then GRPO, and evaluated on the new Video-DR-Bench (200 multi-hop VQA instances). Result: the 35B-A3B Video-DeepResearch model hits 64.0% average accuracy, beating proprietary Claude-4.5-Sonnet (59.0%), GPT-5 (52.5%), and Gemini 2.5 Pro (57.5%) on this benchmark; a smaller 30B-A3B variant reaches 59.3%, competitive with Claude-4.5-Sonnet.

Paper13 | ReflectRL: Learning from Golden Negative Trajectories via Reflective-to-Direct Reasoning · 2026-08-05 01:40 北京时间
Problem: on-policy RL post-training often leans on expert "golden" trajectories, but when the expert itself fails on hard problems, those failed trajectories are usually just discarded as negative samples and their signal is lost. Approach: ReflectRL treats these "Golden Negative Trajectories" as flawed traces to reflect on rather than imitate, first eliciting reflective reasoning from them, then transferring that learned behavior back into direct (non-reflective) reasoning via a lightweight plug-and-play framework. Result: consistent reasoning improvements across 9 benchmarks, 4 LLM backbones, and 4 on-policy training methods, with minimal added overhead.
问题:在线策略强化学习的后训练常常依赖专家模型给出的"金标准"轨迹,但当专家模型自己在难题上也失败时,这些失败轨迹通常被当作负样本直接丢弃,其中的信号也就浪费了。方法:ReflectRL 把这些"金标准失败轨迹"当作值得反思而非值得模仿的错误示例,先从中引导出"反思式推理",再通过一个轻量级即插即用框架,把学到的能力迁移回不带反思的"直接推理"。结果:在 9 个基准、4 种 LLM 基座模型和 4 种在线策略训练方法上都取得了一致的推理能力提升,且额外开销很小。

Paper14 | Should We Type or Talk to LLM Agents? A Comprehensive Study of Voice and Keyboard Input Perturbations · 2026-08-05 01:38 北京时间
Problem: users reach LLMs by typing or speaking, and each channel introduces different noise (keyboard typos vs. voice transcription disfluency/restructuring), but how this actually affects model performance was unclear. Approach: the authors build HIVE, a suite of voice-transcription and QWERTY-keyboard perturbations, and test instruction-tuned models against both. Result: voice perturbations hurt accuracy more than keyboard ones — the damage tracks how many original tokens survive the perturbation, not how many are added; the gap only shows up on open-ended (not multiple-choice) answers; a bigger "thinking budget" almost fully recovers the keyboard channel but barely helps the voice channel.
问题:用户通过打字或说话与 LLM 交互,两种输入渠道会引入不同类型的噪声(键盘打字的拼写错误,语音转写的不流畅和语序重组),但这对模型表现究竟有多大影响此前并不清楚。方法:作者构建了 HIVE 扰动工具集,涵盖语音转写扰动和 QWERTY 键盘输入扰动,并测试多个指令微调模型在两种扰动下的表现。结果:语音类扰动比键盘类扰动伤害更大——伤害程度取决于原始 token 有多少"幸存"下来,而不是新增了多少噪声 token;这一差距只在开放式作答(而非选择题)中才会显现;增加"思考预算"几乎能完全弥补键盘输入的损失,但对语音输入几乎没有帮助。

Paper15 | Information-Geometric Forward Policy Training in GFlowNets · 2026-08-05 01:34 北京时间
Problem: GFlowNets (a framework for amortized inference over discrete/mixed objects via a reward-defined target) lack a principled account of the geometry underlying forward-policy training.
